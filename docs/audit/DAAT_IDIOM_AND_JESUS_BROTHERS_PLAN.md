## Genuine gaps to fill

### Item 3 — Gen 2-3 *da'at tov va-ra* idiom anchoring

1. **2 Samuel 14:17, 20** parallel — woman of Tekoa to David: "as the angel of God to discern good and evil ... my lord has wisdom according to the wisdom of the angel of God, to know all that is on earth." **The strongest Davidic authority-idiom parallel; not currently cited.**
2. **1 Kings 3:9** parallel — Solomon: "give your servant therefore an understanding heart to judge your people, that I may discern between good and evil." **The strongest royal-judicial parallel; not currently cited.** Same merism *tov va-ra* in explicit judicial context.
3. **Ramban (Nachmanides)** on *da'at* — medieval Jewish gloss that *da'at* in Gen 2-3 carries connotations of will / intention, not informational knowledge. Currently absent from §F1.

### Item 1 — Brothers-of-Jesus three-positions naming

1. **Naming the three historical positions** explicitly: **Helvidian** (biological siblings; Protestant majority), **Hieronymian** (cousins; Catholic standard from Jerome), **Epiphanian** (Joseph's children from prior marriage; Eastern Orthodox + Protoevangelium of James). Currently the Yeshua entry uses generic phrasing without naming the historical positions.

### Deliberately NOT in scope

- The two specific Epiphanian arguments (sibling-authority cultural marker from Mark 3:21 / John 7:3-5; John 19:26-27 entrustment): these need chapter-context (Matt 12:46-50, Matt 13:55-56, John 19) — defer to authoring of those chapters.
- Any expansion of Iakobos see-only stub (preserve see-only convention per v3.3.2).
- Last-Adam typology (1 Cor 15:45, Romans 5) from item 3's final third: not Gen 2-3 content; lives in Pauline epistles when authored.
- Bruce Waltke / Tremper Longman / John Walton citations: Walton already in Gen 2 §H Sources. Adding the others requires holding the books in hand to verify specific page-level quotes — not worth the effort for an idiom whose substance is already established by the Hebrew Bible parallels themselves.

## Decision questions (please audit)

### Q1 — Where does the moral-authority idiom textual-parallel entry land?

- **A (recommended):** New entry in **Gen 2 §D (Linguistic Deep Dives)**, numbered D5 or after the existing D-series. Title: "*da'at tov va-ra* as Hebrew-Bible idiom for moral-judicial authority." Cross-references 2 Sam 14:17, 20 + 1 Kgs 3:9 + Deut 1:39 + 2 Sam 19:36.
- **B:** Sharpen existing **Gen 2 §G5** ("Knowing good and evil as a philosophical problem") by adding the 2 Sam 14 + 1 Kgs 3 parallels in-line.
- **C:** Both — new §D entry as the textual-parallel anchor, and revise G5 to cross-reference it.
- **D:** Place in Gen 3 instead (since the idiom is named only in Gen 2:9, 17 but actualized in Gen 3:5, 22).

**My recommendation: A.** §D is the right home for a Hebrew-Bible-wide idiom analysis. §G5 already does the philosophical work and would become muddled if the textual-parallel material is folded in. A is cleanest. **C** is a tighter alternative if you want explicit linkage.

### Q2 — Do we add the Ramban citation to Gen 3 §F1?

If we can't verify the specific quote (the YouTube speaker may have paraphrased), skip this.
- **B:** No — adding a single medieval citation without page-level verification violates our source-attribution standard.

**My recommendation: A with the verification caveat.** If verification fails, fall back to B and note in editorial log.

### Q3 — Where does the brothers-of-Jesus three-positions naming land?
