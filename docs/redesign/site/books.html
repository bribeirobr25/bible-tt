The Books.

Genesis 1–12 · John 1–3 · Matthew 1–3 — each with chapters, study companions, introductions, and people.

Three books, four languages

Genesis
Hebrew Bible · 12 chapters available
Read now →
What
The origin story — of the cosmos, of humanity, and of the family that becomes Israel.
When
Set from creation to Israel’s descent into Egypt; reaching final form by ~6th–5th c. BCE.
Who
Traditionally Moses; critical scholarship sees a composite of older sources edited over centuries.
Why
To ground Israel’s identity, covenant, and promises in primeval and ancestral history.
John
Greek Scriptures · 3 chapters available
Read now →
What
A theological gospel — the logos made flesh, signs, and long discourses.
When
Late first century CE; the latest-composed of the four gospels by most reckonings.
Who
Attributed to the disciple John; authorship and community debated.
Why
“That you may trust” — written to persuade, not merely to report.
Matthew
Greek Scriptures · 3 chapters available
Read now →
What
A gospel rooted in the Hebrew scriptures — genealogy, fulfilment, and teaching.
When
Late first century CE; drawing on earlier sources.
Who
Attributed to Matthew the tax-collector; authorship debated.
Why
To present Yeshua as the continuation of Israel’s story, “kingdom of the skies.”
