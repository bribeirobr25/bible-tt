# Mark 1 — source-analysis notes (Greek)

**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`. First worked example in the Greek corpus; establishes the per-lexeme pattern for GS-source books. Base text NA28; variant data from its apparatus.

**Macro (1:1–3):** No verb in v.1 — a verbless superscription/title (*Archē tou euangeliou…*). The opening citation (vv.2–3) is composite: Mal 3:1 / Exod 23:20 (v.2) + Isa 40:3 (v.3), all attributed to "Isaiah." The narrative engine is *euthys* ("immediately"), 11× in the chapter; macro-pace is the dominant structural feature and must survive into the rendering.

### Archē — Ἀρχή (1:1)
- **Morphology:** nominative singular, anarthrous, no verb. Functions as a heading.
- **Range:** "beginning / origin / first principle / rule." Same word opens LXX Gen 1:1 (*en archē*) and John 1:1 — an intertextual signal of "beginning."
- **Traditional rendering:** "The beginning" adds the article; Greek is anarthrous.
- **Literal:** "Beginning of the good-news…" **Smoothed:** "Beginning of the good news…" (no article, per the anarthrous form).
- **TT status:** encoded — Mark 1:1 (editorial log).

### euangelion — εὐαγγέλιον (1:1,14,15)
- **Morphology:** neuter noun; *eu-* ("good") + *angel-* ("message/announcement").
- **Range:** "good news / good announcement." First-century imperial usage: announcements of an emperor's accession/victory. Not the later genre-label "gospel."
- **Traditional rendering:** "gospel" — a transliteration-by-tradition that hides the plain "good news" and the imperial resonance.
- **TT status:** locked glossary (*euangelion* = good news).

### Christos — Χριστός (1:1)
- **Morphology:** verbal adjective of *chriō* ("anoint") → "anointed (one)." Translates Hebrew *mashiach*.
- **Range:** titular "anointed one"; by Mark's time tending toward a quasi-name for Yeshua, but the titular sense is live in 1:1.
- **Traditional rendering:** "Christ" (transliteration) loses the meaning "anointed."
- **TT status:** locked glossary (*Christos* = the anointed one). Matches Matthew precedent.

### [huiou theou] — υἱοῦ θεοῦ (1:1)
- **Text-critical:** bracketed in NA28. Absent: א*, Θ, 28, a.o. Present: א¹, B, D, L, W. Genuinely split; could be original title or early expansion.
- **Confidence:** UNCERTAIN. Retain (NA28 prints it), flag the dispute. Do not silently drop or assert.
- **TT status:** main text + 🔴 CRITICAL note; editorial log 2026-06-17-001.

### euthys — εὐθύς (1:10,12,18,20,21,23,28,29,30,42,43)
- **Morphology:** adverb, "straightaway / immediately."
- **Range:** temporal immediacy; Mark's signature connective, far denser here than in Matthew/Luke.
- **Decision:** preserve at *every* occurrence — it is a macro-structural feature, not a stylistic tic to vary away. (Refrain-fidelity logic.)
- **TT status:** glossary; editorial log.

### schizomenous — σχιζομένους (1:10)
- **Morphology:** present passive participle of *schizō* ("split/tear/rend"), accusative plural agreeing with *ouranous* ("skies").
- **Range:** "being torn/split" — violent, unlike Matthew's *ēneōchthēsan* ("were opened," 3:16). The same root tears the temple curtain (*eschisthē*, 15:38) — an inclusio framing the Gospel.
- **Traditional rendering:** "opened" (harmonizing to Matthew) loses the violence and the 15:38 link.
- **TT status:** "torn open"; editorial log; cross-ref 15:38 when authored.

### pneuma — πνεῦμα (1:8,10,12 vs 1:23,26,27)
- **Morphology:** neuter noun; "wind / breath / spirit."
- **Range:** the full *ruach* range. **Two contexts:** (a) divine/cosmic spirit (1:8 holy; 1:10 descent; 1:12 driving-out) → genuine wind/spirit ambiguity, **slash retained** (Rule 2); (b) *pneuma akatharton* "unclean spirit" (1:23,26,27) → demonic agent; "unclean wind" is not a live sense, **slash dropped**.
- **Decision:** context-gated slash. Preserve ambiguity only where it is genuine (Rule 2), not mechanically.
- **TT status:** {a:wind/spirit} (divine) vs "unclean spirit" (demonic); editorial log.

### ekballei — ἐκβάλλει (1:12)
- **Morphology:** present active indicative, 3sg ("drives/throws out"); historical present.
- **Range:** forceful expulsion — the same verb used for casting out demons (1:34,39). Matthew/Luke soften to "led up / led."
- **Traditional rendering:** "led" loses the force; the Spirit *expels* Yeshua.
- **TT status:** "drives … out" (present preserved); editorial log.

### aphesis — ἄφεσις (1:4)
- **Morphology:** noun from *aphiēmi* ("send away / let go / release / forgive").
- **Range:** "release / dismissal / remission." "Forgiveness" is the conventional theological narrowing of a concrete "release/sending-away."
- **Confidence:** PROBABLE — keep concrete "release" in main text, note the conventional sense; flag for Hellenist.
- **TT status:** "release of sins" + Tier-2 note; editorial log.

### splanchnistheis / orgistheis — σπλαγχνισθείς / ὀργισθείς (1:41)
- **Text-critical:** NA28 *splanchnistheis* ("moved with compassion," from *splanchna*, "inward parts/bowels" — the seat of compassion). Variant *orgistheis* ("moved with anger," D + Old Latin) is the *lectio difficilior*; some editors prefer it precisely because scribes would soften anger → compassion, not the reverse.
- **Confidence:** UNCERTAIN. Follow NA28 ("compassion"); flag the harder reading.
- **TT status:** "moved with compassion" + 🔴 CRITICAL note; editorial log.

### lepra / lepros — λέπρα / λεπρός (1:40,42)
- **Morphology:** noun / adjective; "scaly condition / scaly-one."
- **Range:** a **broad category of skin/surface affliction** = HB *tsara'at* (Lev 13–14), **not** modern Hansen's disease. Carries ritual-impurity and social-exclusion weight.
- **Traditional rendering:** "leprosy/leper" imports the modern clinical disease — anachronistic.
- **Confidence:** PROBABLE. Keep "leper/leprosy" (familiar) + Tier-2 category note; flag.
- **TT status:** "leper / leprosy" + note; editorial log.
