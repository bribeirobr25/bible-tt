# 1 Peter — source-analysis notes (Greek)

**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`. Base text NA28; variant data from its apparatus. **Rule 28:** AI-assisted draft pending Hellenist review.

**Macro:** 1 Peter is the corpus's **first epistle** (genre pilot) — a circular letter of *hope under suffering* to "elect exiles of the dispersion" across five provinces of Asia Minor (1:1; provinces overlapping the Acts 2:9 pilgrim list). By **Kefa (Peter)**, "through Silvanus" (5:12); "Mark my son" + "she in Babylon" (5:13). Salutation (1:1-2) → body (new birth/hope → holiness → the household & social codes → suffering for the name) → closing (5:12-14). Text is comparatively stable (no Acts-scale Western divergence). The letter *deploys* the Hebrew Bible paraenetically and typologically (OT-in-argument), not as "prediction → fulfillment."

### Key lexemes / decisions
- **κύριος (*kyrios*)** — Option C. YHWH-referent in the OT citations: 1:25 (Isa 40:8), 2:3 (Ps 34:8), 3:12 (Ps 34:15-16). **3:15 referent-shift**: Isa 8:13 "the LORD of hosts… sanctify him" → "sanctify the Messiah as Lord" (YHWH-text applied to Christ; Rule 13 + Tier-2; cf. Acts 2:36). 3:6 Sarah "calling him lord" = a *human* honorific (lowercase).
- **πνεῦμα** — {a:wind/spirit} for the divine/Spirit + the flesh/spirit-sphere crux (1:2,11,12; 3:18; 4:6,14); rendered **plain "spirit(s)"** where no "wind" sense is live (3:4 disposition; 3:19 imprisoned beings).
- **Rule 30:** little/no narrated divine speech (the letter is the apostle's exhortation). Marked `@@…@@` only where an OT citation is God's first-person word: **1:16** (Lev "I am holy"), **2:6** (Isa 28:16 "I lay in Zion"). The Ps 34, Isa 40, Isa 8, Prov, Hosea citations are human (prophet/psalmist/sage) voice → unmarked.
- **Identity keyword:** παροίκους/παρεπιδήμους/διασπορά/παροικία (1:1,17; 2:11) = "resident-aliens/sojourners/exiles/dispersion."
- Locked GS terms match the corpus: Χριστός "Messiah", ἀναγεννάω "born anew", ὑποτάσσω "submit", ξύλον "tree" (2:24), ἐπίσκοπος/ποιμήν (2:25; 5:2,4), βάπτισμα "immersion".

### Cruxes for Rule-28 review (the Hellenist's headline items)
- **3:18–20 "the spirits in prison"** (P-006): flesh/spirit datives + 3 readings (Noah's-day dead / Genesis-6 watchers [Enochic, cf. 2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6] / pre-incarnate proclamation) — preserved, resolved none.
- **3:21 baptism "now saves you… as antitype"** (P-007): flood antitype; ἐπερώτημα "appeal/pledge"; "through the resurrection" — no sacramental import.
- **4:6 "the gospel preached to the dead"** (P-010): linked to 3:19; readings flagged, resolved none.
- **1:1–2 salutation** (P-008): the Father/spirit/blood triune-shaped election — rendered without later dogmatic import (Rule 3).
- **2:18–3:7 household code** (P-009): servants; wives/husbands; ὑποτάσσω; "weaker vessel" + "co-heir of grace"; Sarah — the highest-restraint passage; render-and-flag, no position (Rule 3). The likely reviewer flashpoint.
- **2:4–8 stone catena** (Isa 28:16 + Ps 118:22 + Isa 8:14); **2:8** ἐτέθησαν "appointed" (election); **2:9–10** Israel's-titles transfer (Ex 19:6 / Hosea — no supersessionist gloss).
