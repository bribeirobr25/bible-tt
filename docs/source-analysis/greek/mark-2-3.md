# Mark 2–3 — source-analysis notes (Greek)

**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`. Base text NA28; variant data from its apparatus.

**Macro (2:1–3:6):** five linked controversy stories building to the death-plot (3:6) — a deliberate Markan unit. Macro of 3:20–35: an *intercalation* (family frame 20–21 / 31–35 around the Beelzevul dispute 22–30), where "house/kingdom divided" (24–25) echoes the "house" of the family frame.

### ho huios tou anthrōpou — ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου (2:10, 2:28)
- **Morphology:** articular "the son of the human/man"; Greek calque of Aramaic *bar (e)nash*.
- **Range:** (a) generic "a human being"; (b) self-reference idiom; (c) the exalted figure of **Daniel 7:13** ("one like a son of man"). Mark's first uses already attach divine prerogatives (forgive sins; lord of the sabbath), tilting titular.
- **Decision:** keep "Son of Man" (familiar/established) + Tier-2 range note; do not collapse to "human being" or to "the Messiah". The 2:27→2:28 inference works on either sense — preserve the openness.
- **TT status:** glossary; editorial log 2026-06-18-002.

### aphiēmi (verb) — ἀφίενται / ἀφιέναι (2:5, 2:9, 2:7)
- **Morphology:** from *apo* + *hiēmi*, "send away / let go / release / forgive." 2:5 present passive ("are forgiven/released").
- **Range:** the passive may be a *divine passive* (God forgives) — which is exactly what the scribes contest (2:7, "who can forgive but God?").
- **Decision:** "forgiven" in main text (readable), release sense in note; verbal counterpart of the noun *aphesis* "release" (1:4). Flag consistency for Hellenist.

### pneuma — πνεῦμα (2:8 vs 3:29 vs 3:30)
- **2:8 "in his spirit"** — Yeshua's own inner spirit/perception; "wind" not a live sense → **no slash**.
- **3:29 "the holy spirit"** — the divine spirit → **slash kept** ({a:wind/spirit}), as in 1:8.
- **3:30 "unclean spirit"** — demonic agent → **no slash**.
- **Principle:** context-gated slash (Rule 2 preserves *genuine* ambiguity only). Three different *pneuma* contexts, three different renderings, all logged.

### epi Abiathar archiereōs — ἐπὶ Ἀβιαθὰρ ἀρχιερέως (2:26)
- **Crux:** 1 Sam 21 names **Ahimelech** (Abiathar's father) as the priest. Long-recognized discrepancy. Some witnesses (D, W, 1009 a.o.) omit "in the time of Abiathar the high priest", easing it.
- **Decision:** render the NA28 text; flag 🔴 CRITICAL; do not harmonize to 1 Samuel. Confidence: UNCERTAIN.

### sabbaton … dia ton anthrōpon — τὸ σάββατον διὰ τὸν ἄνθρωπον ἐγένετο (2:27)
- *anthrōpos* generic ("the human", humanity), not male-specific. *egeneto* "came into being" → the sabbath's purpose is human benefit (Gen 2:2–3; Exod 20:8–11). 2:28 *kyrios* = "lord/master" (NOT the divine name; no OT quotation here).

### epoiēsen dōdeka — ἐποίησεν δώδεκα (3:14–16)
- "He made twelve" — number evokes the twelve tribes (reconstitution motif, inferred not stated).
- **Variant:** "[whom he also named *apostolous*]" bracketed in NA28 (א, B, C* vs. omission; possible harmonization to Luke 6:13). Render in italic-brackets; flag.
- Names: Semitic forms transliterated (Kefa, Bar-Talmai, Mattai, Toma, Taddai, Yehudah Ish-Keriot). **Σίμων ὁ Καναναῖος** = from Aramaic *qan'ana* ("zealous") = Zealot (cf. Luke 6:15 *zēlōtēs*), NOT "from Cana." **Βοανηργές** glossed by Mark himself ("sons of thunder") — keep transliteration + Mark's gloss.

### sy ei ho huios tou theou — σὺ εἶ ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ θεοῦ (3:11)
- Spoken by **unclean spirits** — non-divine speaker → **not** Rule-30 divine speech (parallel to 1:24). Ordinary quotation; silenced by the messianic-secret command (3:12).

### blasphēmia eis to pneuma — βλασφημία εἰς τὸ πνεῦμα τὸ ἅγιον (3:28–30)
- *aphesis eis ton aiōna* "no forgiveness for the age/forever"; *hamartēma aiōnion* "eternal sin." The narrator **defines** the blasphemy (3:30): calling the holy spirit's work an unclean spirit. Slash kept on the holy *pneuma* (3:29). Render the severity without softening. Confidence: VERIFIED.
