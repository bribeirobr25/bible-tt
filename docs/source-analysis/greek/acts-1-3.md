# Acts 1–3 — source-analysis notes (Greek)

**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`. Base text NA28; variant data from its apparatus. **Rule 28:** AI-assisted draft pending Hellenist review.

**Macro:** Acts is the second volume of Luke (1:1, "the first account"; same author, same dedicatee Theophilos), continuing from the ascension. Acts 1 = ascension + the reconstitution of the Twelve (Matthias by lot). Acts 2 = Pentecost/Shavuot, the Spirit, and the first kerygmatic sermon (Joel 2; Ps 16; Ps 110). Acts 3 = the temple healing at the Beautiful Gate + the second sermon (the Servant; Deut 18; Gen 22). Heavily OT-citational — the densest fulfillment material per chapter in the GS so far.

**Textual note (Acts-specific):** Acts has the NT's **largest Alexandrian (NA28) vs Western (Codex Bezae D) divergence**. Chs 1–3 variants are modest but real (e.g. the bracketed "[ἔγειρε καὶ]" / "rise and" at 3:6). The TT follows NA28; significant variants → Tier-2.

### Key lexemes / decisions (to expand during review)
- **κύριος (*kyrios*)** — Divine-Name Option C (RULES-GS). YHWH-referent in the citations: Joel "day of the Lord" / "calls on the name of the Lord" (2:20-21); Ps 16:8 "I saw the Lord" (2:25); Ps 110:1 (2:34); "the face of the Lord" / "the Lord God" (3:19-20, 22). **2:34 is the headline crux**: the double-κύριος ("*The Lord* [YHWH] said to my *lord* [the Messiah]") — two referents under one Greek word; Rule 13 + Tier-2 (acts.md A-005).
- **πνεῦμα (*pneuma*)** — locked {a:wind/spirit} slash; the promise (1:5,8), the Pentecost outpouring (2), the power behind the signs (3).
- **γλῶσσαι (*glōssai*)** — {a:tongues/languages} (2:4,11) where both senses are live; contrast διάλεκτος "language" (2:6,8). The fire-shape "tongues" (2:3) is the body-part sense, not the slash.
- **πεντηκοστή (*pentēkostē*)** — "Pentecost," glossed Shavuot / Feast of Weeks (2:1).
- **μετάνοια / ἄφεσις** — "change of mind" / "release-forgiveness" (2:38; 3:19); the **εἰς** of 2:38 ("for/unto the release of sins") preserved, not resolved (purpose vs ground — Uncertain).
- **παῖς (*pais*)** — "servant" (the Isaianic Servant, Isa 52-53) of 3:13, 26; child/servant range noted.
- **ἀρχηγός (*archēgos*)** — "Author/Pioneer/Originator of life" (3:15); full range intended.
- **ἀποκατάστασις (*apokatastasis*)** — "restoration of all things" (3:21); scope debated, rendered literally.
- **Ἁκελδαμάχ (*Hakeldamach*)** — Aramaic {t:Hakeldama}, "Field of Blood" (1:19), glossed by the text itself.
- **Rule 30 (divine speech):** risen Yeshua (1:4-8); the Joel oracle first-person divine speech (2:17-21); the LORD's command in Ps 110 (2:34-35); the LORD's covenant word to Avraham (Gen 22:18, 3:25). NOT marked: the ascension angels (1:10-11, *angelos*-class); Moses's reported speech about the LORD (Deut 18, 3:22-23); the sermons (human speech).
