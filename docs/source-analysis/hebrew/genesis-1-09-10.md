# Genesis 1:9–10 — source-analysis notes (Day 3, part 1)

**Source (v9):** וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יִקָּווּ הַמַּיִם מִתַּחַת הַשָּׁמַיִם אֶל־מָקוֹם אֶחָד וְתֵרָאֶה הַיַּבָּשָׁה וַיְהִי־כֵן
**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`.

### yikkavu — יִקָּווּ
- **Morphology:** future, plural (the final *vav* marks plural; *mayim* "water" is grammatically plural). Future-as-description, not the imperative (an imperative "gather!" form exists but is unused here — God describes the outcome rather than ordering the water).
- **Range:** root tied to **bodies of water** specifically; "be collected/gathered." (Related noun *miqveh*, v10.)
- **Smoothed:** "the water will be gathered" (no "let," which is not in Hebrew).

### mitachat ha-shamayim — מִתַּחַת הַשָּׁמַיִם
- *mi-* "from" + *tachat* "under" + *ha-shamayim* "the sky." With *asher* absent, reads "under the sky." Best: "under the sky," the primeval sea lying low.

### el maqom echad — אֶל־מָקוֹם אֶחָד
- **el** — preposition "to" (motion); the one place in the chapter expressing actual movement. **Homographs to keep distinct** (same consonants): *al* "don't" (e.g. Gen 22:12), *el* "to," and *El* "a god" (in *Beth-El*, *Israel*, *El Al*). Coincidental, since Hebrew writes no vowels.
- **maqom** — "place"; usually a limited/specific location, but here used for a vast expanse (unusual).
- **echad** — "one." → "to one place."

### teira'eh ha-yabashah — וְתֵרָאֶה הַיַּבָּשָׁה
- **teira'eh** — root *r-ʾ-h* "see," **passive** ("be seen"); better than active "appear" (which Hebrew has no special word for). Future, no converter → the dry land has been visible ever since (not just to God; to anyone).
- **yabashah** — root *y-b-sh* "to dry" → "the dry [ground]"; the word names *dryness*, not "land" specifically (cf. continents/temporary dry crossings). Best: "dry ground."
- **vayhi-ken** — "and it was so" (see 1:7).
- **TT status:** passive preserved (Rule 8); "be seen"; "dry ground."

# Genesis 1:10

**Source:** וַיִּקְרָא אֱלֹהִים לַיַּבָּשָׁה אֶרֶץ וּלְמִקְוֵה הַמַּיִם קָרָא יַמִּים וַיַּרְא אֱלֹהִים כִּי־טוֹב

### eretz / la-yabashah — אֶרֶץ / לַיַּבָּשָׁה
- God names the dry ground *eretz* "land" (anarthrous *eretz*; with the article it becomes *ha-aretz*). Reinforces *eretz* = "land," not "Earth/planet" (see `genesis-1-01.md`).

### miqveh ha-mayim — מִקְוֵה הַמַּיִם
- **Morphology:** *u-le-* ("and to-the") + *miqveh* + *ha-mayim*. Root shared with *yikkavu* (v9) — **water-collection** sense (cf. Exod 7:19; Lev 11:36; and the Jewish ritual bath *mikveh*). → "the gathering/body of the water."

### yamim — יַמִּים
- **Morphology:** **plural** "seas" (singular *yam*). Plural *yamim* is rare and mostly poetic; here (and v22) it is non-poetic.
- **Note (geography, not linguistics):** from an ANE vantage there were effectively two physically separated seas (Mediterranean = "the great sea"; Red Sea), yet v9 says the water gathers "to one place" — a tension worth flagging, not resolving.
- **ki tov** — "that good" (see 1:4); closes with the satisfaction formula. Here the unnamed referent is plural (land + seas), so "it was good" is a fair compromise for grammatical English.
- **TT status:** *yamim* "seas"; *ki tov* formula.
