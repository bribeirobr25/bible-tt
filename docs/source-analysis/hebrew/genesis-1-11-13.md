# Genesis 1:11–13 — source-analysis notes (Day 3, part 2)

**Source (v11):** וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים תַּדְשֵׁא הָאָרֶץ דֶּשֶׁא עֵשֶׂב מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע עֵץ פְּרִי עֹשֶׂה פְּרִי לְמִינוֹ אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ עַל־הָאָרֶץ וַיְהִי־כֵן
**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`.

**Macro:** vv11–12 introduce a major stylistic shift — heavy synonym-stringing and root-doubling, and a command (v11) closely mirrored by its outcome (v12). Hard to render; the awkwardness is the point.

### tadshe — תַּדְשֵׁא
- **Morphology:** a **denominal verb** from *deshe* "grass" → "to grass over"; **feminine** (*Tav* prefix) agreeing with *ha-aretz* (feminine). The land grasses over itself.
- **Smoothed:** "the land shall grass over" (EN has a rare verb "to grass over" — near-exact match).

### deshe / esev — דֶּשֶׁא / עֵשֶׂב
- Two near-synonyms strung **without a connector** (poetic doubling). *deshe* = naturally-occurring grass (linked to rain/drought, e.g. Jer 14:5). *esev* = grass/herbage; may extend to crops (Ps 104:14), so rendered "herbs" when a second English word is needed.
- **Smoothed:** "grass, herbs" (closest feel: "grassy herbs").

### mazria zera — מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע
- **Root-doubling:** *mazria* "seeding" (adj./participle) + *zera* "seed" → "seeding seed" = producing/yielding seed. A standard biblical device (form + cognate noun adjacent); preserve it.

### etz pri / oseh pri — עֵץ פְּרִי / עֹשֶׂה פְּרִי
- **etz** "tree" (singular as a **collective**; plural would be *etzim*). **pri** "fruit" (always singular, like EN "fruit"). *oseh pri* "making fruit." So "fruit tree making fruit" — the repetition of "fruit" is intentional, not redundant to trim.

### lemino — לְמִינוֹ
- **Morphology:** *le-* "to/of" + *min* "kind/type" + *-o* possessive ("its"). → "of its kind." (KJV "after his kind": "his" misleads — trees are inanimate; Hebrew has no neuter, only m/f, and *etz*/*pri* are masculine.)
- **Attestation:** *min* + suffix appears in Gen 1 and the clean/unclean-animal laws (Leviticus, Deuteronomy).

### asher zar'o-vo — אֲשֶׁר זַרְעוֹ־בוֹ
- *asher* "which/that" + *zar'o* ("its seed," third use of the *z-r-ʿ* root in the verse) + *bo* ("in it," *be-* + suffix). → "whose seed is in it." Logically redundant (all fruit has seed) — likely a fertility emphasis / stylistic.

### al-ha-aretz / vayhi-ken
- *al* "on" + *ha-aretz* "the land/ground" → "on the land"; *vayhi-ken* "and it was so."

# Genesis 1:12–13

**v12** repeats v11 as **outcome** (a new device: significant repetition of the command). Key changes:
- **totze — וַתּוֹצֵא** — root *y-tz-ʾ* "bring/go out," converting-vav, feminine → "and the land **brought out**" (active: the ground brings forth the vegetation).
- *lemino* appears twice (as *le-mino* / *le-minehu* variants — same meaning).
- closes with *vayar Elohim ki tov* — "and God saw that good."

**v13** — *vayhi-erev vayhi-voker yom shlishi* — "and was evening and was morning, third day" (*shlishi* ordinal; cf. cardinal *echad* at 1:5). The closing-frame's third occurrence.
