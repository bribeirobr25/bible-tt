# Genesis 1:6–8 — source-analysis notes (Day 2)

**Source (v6):** וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי רָקִיעַ בְּתוֹךְ הַמָּיִם וִיהִי מַבְדִּיל בֵּין מַיִם לָמָיִם
**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`.

**Macro:** Much vocabulary recurs from vv1–5. The key word is *raqia* (×5 across these verses); a dedicated investigation follows the lexemes.

### raqia — רָקִיעַ
- **Morphology:** noun; root **r-q-ʿ** = "to hammer / beat / flatten" (metal-work sense; cf. Exod 39 hammered plates; Ezek 6:11 stamping).
- **Attestation:** 15× in 4 books (Genesis, Ezekiel, Psalms, Daniel). In Genesis/Psalms/Daniel ≈ synonymous with *shamayim* "sky." Ezekiel 1: a *raqia* over the creatures' heads, like awesome ice/frost, "stretched/spread" (*natui*).
- **Reading:** probably a solid, hammered/pressed structure (at least figuratively) — not necessarily dome-shaped, though plausible from the night-sky appearance. **Not** "firmament" (medieval solid-dome import) and **not** flatly "expanse" (loses the root).
- **Confidence:** **Uncertain** (exact referent debated).
- **TT status:** transliterated *raqia* (Rule 4; transliteration-decisions log); gloss "(expanse)" at first occurrence.

### betoch — בְּתוֹךְ
- **Morphology:** *be-* "in" + *toch* "midst/inside."
- **Range:** best = "inside / within" (not "in the midst of"). Evidence: *betoch* most often = within/inside (cf. Mic 3:3 meat inside a cauldron). So *raqia* is made **within** the water — initially submerged.
- **TT status:** SHIPPED 2026-06-04 — Gen 1:6 Tier 1 now reads "within the waters" (all 4 locales) + v6 note rewritten + companion §A4 harmonized; see genesis.md Entry 2026-06-04-111 (Q6 §2.1 reconciliation).

### mavdil — מַבְדִּיל
- **Morphology:** root *b-d-l*; **not** the infinitive (which would be *le-havdil*) → here a **noun**, "a separator/divider," not the verb "to separate."
- **Smoothed:** "shall be a separator." (Subtle but real: separator vs. to-separate.)
- **TT status:** SHIPPED 2026-06-04 — Gen 1:6 Tier 1 now reads "a separator" (EN) / "um separador" (PT) / "eine Scheidewand" (DE — locale-editor confirmation pending) / "un separador" (ES); v6 note updated (substantival Hiphil participle); see genesis.md Entry 2026-06-04-111 (Q6 §2.2 reconciliation).

### bein mayim la-mayim — בֵּין מַיִם לָמָיִם
- *bein* "between" + *mayim* "water," then *la-mayim* ("to-the water"); the *bein…uvein* frame here uses *le-* not a second *bein*. By the *nikud*: "between water to-the-water." Drop the pointing and it reads "between water to water." Two bodies of water are implied.

### vaya'as / asher / mitachat / me'al / ken (vv7–8)
- **vaya'as** — root *ʿ-s-h* "make/do"; short form + converting-vav → "and he made."
- **asher** — "which / that"; tense-neutral (so the water's position need not be locked to past).
- **mitachat / me'al** — "from under" / "from above" (*me-* "from" + *tachat*/*al*). Frame: water under the *raqia* vs. above it.
- **ken** — "so / thus" (NOT "yes"; biblical Hebrew has no word for "yes" — affirmation repeats the key word). "And it was so."
- **shamayim** (v8) — God names the *raqia* "sky"; so *raqia* ≈ *shamayim* here (see `genesis-1-01.md`).
- **sheni** — "second" (ordinal) → "second day" (contrast *yom echad* at 1:5).

### Investigation — what is the raqia?
From the text: created within the water; separates lower from upper water; named "sky." From wider attestation: root = hammer/beat; Ezekiel's *raqia* is ice-like and stretched. Best-supported reading: a solid, beaten structure separating two water-masses, the upper water suspended above it (cf. "windows of the heavens," *arubot ha-shamayim*, opened in the Flood). Shape (dome) is inference, not stated. **The TT transliterates and lets the reader weigh it; it does not resolve the cosmology.**
