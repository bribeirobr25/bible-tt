# Genesis 1:3 — source-analysis notes

**Source:** וַיֹּאמֶר אֱלֹהִים יְהִי אוֹר וַיְהִי־אוֹר
**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`.

**Macro:** Third consecutive sentence opening with *vav*. The command and the outcome use the **same two words** (*yehi or* / *vayhi or*), distinguished only by the converting-*vav* — very likely deliberate artistry. "Let there" in English is an addition with no Hebrew equivalent.

### vayomer — וַיֹּאמֶר
- **Morphology:** root *ʾ-m-r* "say"; *yomar* is future, and the prefixed **converting-vav** flips it to past → "and he said," 3ms. (Drop the *vav* and it reads future; this future↔past converter is the signature feature of biblical Hebrew, later abandoned.)
- **Smoothed:** "And God said."
- **TT status:** converting-vav policy (HB §Converting Vav), explained at first occurrence.

### Elohim — אֱלֹהִים
- See `genesis-1-01.md`. With *vayomer*: "and God said."

### yehi — יְהִי
- **Morphology:** root *h-y-h* "be," **future/jussive** ("shall be"), a shorter, elevated form vs. the standard *yihyeh* ("will be").
- **Range:** command expressed as future, not the dedicated imperative — common in Hebrew. Also carries a wishing/may-it-be sense elsewhere (Ps 122:7).
- **Traditional rendering:** "Let there be" (adds "let there"). **Smoothed (TT):** "shall be."
- **TT status:** jussive policy (HB verb system); "shall be light."

### or — אוֹר
- **Range:** "light" (visible light; not "lightweight/light-touch"). Unchanged biblical→modern (*or kachol* blue light, *or shemesh* sunlight).

### vayhi-or — וַיְהִי־אוֹר
- **Morphology:** same *yehi* + **converting-vav** → past "and was." So command *yehi or* "shall be light" vs. outcome *vayhi or* "and was light" — identical words, the *vav* doing the work.
- **Literal:** "shall be light / and was light." **Smoothed:** "shall be light; and light was." Awkward in English, elegant in Hebrew.
- **TT status:** converting-vav; note the intentional command=outcome repetition.
