# Genesis 1:2 — source-analysis notes

**Source:** וְהָאָרֶץ הָיְתָה תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ וְחֹשֶׁךְ עַל־פְּנֵי תְהוֹם וְרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים מְרַחֶפֶת עַל־פְּנֵי הַמָּיִם
**Method:** `../METHOD.md`. Internal working notes — see `../README.md`.

**Macro:** Long verse, two sentences. Note: English versions show **two** verbs in the second half ("darkness *was* upon…"); Hebrew has only one verb (*haytah*), reused — the "was" before darkness is a translator's addition.

### ve-ha-aretz haytah — וְהָאָרֶץ הָיְתָה
- **Morphology:** *haytah* = "was," 3**fs** (feminine) because *aretz* is feminine. (All Hebrew nouns are gendered; soil/land feminine, planet-sense masculine.)
- **Range:** "and the land was." *aretz* here may point to dry land in a chaotic/non-existent state rather than the planet (reinforced by the water imagery following).
- **TT status:** *eretz* glossary; gender tracked (Rule 27) where loaded.

### tohu va-vohu — תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ
- **Morphology:** rhyming word-pair joined by *va-* "and"; musical/idiomatic.
- **Range:** genuinely uncertain. Appears only 3× (here; Jer 4:23; Isa 34:11). Jeremiah/Isaiah use it in contexts of **ruin and destruction** (Isa 34:11 pairs it with confusion/emptiness of a destroyed city). So it can describe both a primeval world and a ruined habitat — puzzling.
- **Ancient vs. modern:** Modern Hebrew *tohu* ≈ "mess/chaos."
- **Traditional rendering:** "without form and void"; others: "chaos," "desolation," "wasteland." All are interpretive calls.
- **Confidence:** **Uncertain** (rare term, contested even among scholars).
- **TT status:** glossary (*tohu vavohu* = chaos and void, uncertainty flagged).

### choshech — חֹשֶׁךְ
- **Range:** "darkness/dark," straightforward (opposite *or* "light"). Unchanged from biblical to modern Hebrew.

### al-pnei — עַל־פְּנֵי
- **Morphology:** *al* "on" + *pnei* (construct of *panim* "face"). Hyphen marks the two words as one unit.
- **Range:** "on the face of," but largely just "upon" — a higher-register form of *al* (cf. Eccl 11:1 "upon the waters"; Gen 23:19 "before/east of"). "Face of" is often redundant in target idiom.
- **TT status:** idiom policy; render "upon" / "on the face of" per context, note the idiom.

### tehom — תְהוֹם
- **Morphology:** noun; appears in plural *tehomot* for emphasis (Exod 15).
- **Range:** "the deep." Ancient attestation points to a **massive body of water** — sea/ocean/groundwater (Exod 15 frozen sea-water; Gen 7:11 "fountains of the great deep" = groundwater/aquifer). Possible (noted, not adopted) link to Akkadian *Tiamat*.
- **Ancient vs. modern:** Modern Hebrew *tehom* = "chasm/precipice" (drift away from the water sense) — **do not** import the modern sense.
- **Traditional rendering:** "the deep." **Confidence:** water-mass sense **Probable**.
- **TT status:** glossary (*tehom* = the deep; HB↔GS aligned with *abyssos*).

### ve-ruach Elohim — וְרוּחַ אֱלֹהִים
- **Morphology:** *ve-* "and" + *ruach* (feminine) + *Elohim*.
- **Range:** **wind/spirit** — both senses live; ~350× in the Tanakh as wind and as spirit, treated as closely related/interchangeable (Gen 7:15 "breath of life" is a merge point). Not "ghost" (that sense is post-biblical).
- **TT status:** glossary slash (*ruach* = wind/spirit), Rule 2; HB↔GS aligned with *pneuma*; gender mismatch tracked for DE/ES.

### merachefet — מְרַחֶפֶת
- **Morphology:** participle, **feminine** (agreeing with *ruach*), **present** tense — unusually, unlike the surrounding past-tense narration.
- **Range:** "hover / move / flutter / cover." Root appears only 3× (this; Jer 23:9 "trembling/restless"; Deut 32:11 of an eagle — fluttering / hovering / possibly shielding its young). So the motion may be more restless than the serene picture usually drawn.
- **Traditional rendering:** "moved/hovered" — and rendered **past** tense, though Hebrew is present.
- **Confidence:** **Uncertain** (rare root; motion vs. covering debated).
- **TT status:** Tier 2 note (participle aspect + present tense + range).
