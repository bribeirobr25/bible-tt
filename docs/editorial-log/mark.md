## Entry 2026-06-17-001 — Mark activation + chapter 1 pilot (EN)

**Scope:** First book of the multi-book expansion (`docs/audit/MULTI_BOOK_EXPANSION_PLAN.md`). Mark 1 (EN) authored as the full-stack pilot; chapters 2–3, the other three locales, and the chapter companions follow.

**Base text:** NA28. Divine-name policy: GS Option C (*kyrios* → "the Lord" in OT-quotation contexts where the referent is YHWH; noted Tier 2).

### Naming decisions (RULES-GS proper-name convention; first occurrence "Transliterated (Familiar)", then familiar)
- Ἰησοῦς → **Yeshua (Jesus)**. Χριστός → **"the anointed one"** (lowercase, translated — matches Matthew precedent, *not* transliterated "Christ" or "Mashiach").
- Ἰωάννης → **Yochanan (John)** (the Immerser). Ἠσαΐας → **Yesha'yahu (Isaiah)**. Μωϋσῆς → **Moshe (Moses)**.
- Ἰορδάνης → **Yarden (Jordan)**. Ἰουδαία → **Yehudah (Judea)**. Ἱεροσόλυμα → **Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)**. Γαλιλαία → **Galil (Galilee)**. Ναζαρέτ → **Natzeret (Nazareth)**; the adjectival Ναζαρηνέ (1:24) → **Natzeri (of Nazareth)**. Καφαρναούμ → **Kfar Nachum (Capernaum)**.
- Σίμων → **Shimon (Simon)**. Ἀνδρέας → **Andreas (Andrew)**. Ἰάκωβος → **Ya'aqov (James)** (same name as the patriarch Jacob; familiar EN "James"). Ζεβεδαῖος → **Zavdai (Zebedee)**.

### Glossary / lexical decisions (locked-glossary alignment with Matthew/John)
- εὐαγγέλιον → **"good news"** (not "gospel"). βάπτισμα/βαπτίζω → **immersion/immerse** (not "baptism"). μετάνοια/μετανοέω → **change of mind / change *your* minds** (not "repentance"). κηρύσσω → **proclaim**.
- **βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ → "kingdom of God"** (1:15). NOTE: Mark uses "kingdom of God"; Matthew's distinctive "kingdom of the skies" (*tōn ouranōn*) does **not** appear in Mark. The TT renders each Gospel's actual phrase — no harmonizing.
- **πνεῦμα slash policy (Rule 2):** rendered **{a:wind/spirit}** for the divine spirit (1:8 holy spirit, 1:10 descent, 1:12 driving-out) — HB *ruach* alignment. For **πνεῦμα ἀκάθαρτον** ("unclean spirit", 1:23,26,27) the slash is **dropped** — "wind" is not a live sense in the demonic-possession context; rendered "unclean spirit". Logged as a deliberate context-gated exception to the blanket slash, consistent with Rule 2 (preserve *genuine* ambiguity only).
- **ἄφεσις ἁμαρτιῶν (1:4) → "release of sins"** in main text, with Tier-2 note that *aphesis* = release / sending-away / remission (concrete sense preserved over conventional "forgiveness"). Flagged for Hellenist confirmation.
- **σατανᾶς (1:13) → "the Satan"** (transliteration of Hebrew *ha-satan*, "the adversary"); Tier-2 note gives the adversary sense. Rule-3-sensitive (no imported demonology beyond the text); rendered as the text's term without elaboration.
- **λεπρός / λέπρα (1:40,42) → "leper" / "leprosy"** in main text, with Tier-2 note that Greek *lepra* (= HB *tsara'at*) is a broader category of skin/surface affliction, **not** modern Hansen's disease. Flagged.
- **εὐθύς ("immediately") preserved at every occurrence** (1:10,12,18,20,21,23,28,29,30,42,43) — Mark's signature adverb; no stylistic variation.

### Rule 30 (divine speech, `@@…@@`) scope for Mark 1
- **Marked:** God's voice 1:11; Yeshua's speech 1:15, 1:17, 1:25, 1:38, 1:41, 1:44.
- **NOT marked (logged):** the narrator's composite OT citation 1:2–3 (narrator quotation, incl. *kyrios*); the **unclean spirit's** speech 1:24 ("I know who you are — the holy one of God") — a non-divine speaker *about* Yeshua; the crowd 1:27; the disciples 1:37; the leper 1:40.
- **Mark 1:11 is second person** — @@"You are my son…"@@ — contrast Matthew 3:17's third-person "This is my son…". The TT preserves Mark's direct address; no harmonizing to Matthew.

### Text-critical variants pre-registered (RULES-GS variant-significance threshold)
- **1:1 [υἱοῦ θεοῦ] "son of God"** — bracketed/disputed in NA28 (absent from א*, Θ, 28 a.o.; present in א¹, B, D, L, W). Retained in main text (NA28 prints it), flagged 🔴 CRITICAL with the witness split.
- **1:2 "ἐν τῷ Ἠσαΐᾳ τῷ προφήτῃ" ("in Isaiah the prophet")** — NA28 reading; Byzantine reads "in the prophets" (ἐν τοῖς προφήταις), a harmonizing fix because the citation is **composite** (Malachi 3:1 / Exod 23:20 + Isaiah 40:3). Flagged 🔴 CRITICAL; the composite source is the reason for the variant.
- **1:41 σπλαγχνισθείς ("moved with compassion") vs ὀργισθείς ("moved with anger")** — NA28 reads "compassion"; the "anger" reading (Codex Bezae D + Old Latin) is the *lectio difficilior* and is text-critically significant. Flagged 🔴 CRITICAL with both readings.

**Status:** provisional. Reviewers unassigned. Source-analysis corpus entry: `docs/source-analysis/greek/mark-1.md`.

## Entry 2026-06-18-002 — Mark 2–3 (EN)

**Scope:** Mark 2 (28 v.) and Mark 3 (35 v.), EN, full chapter depth. Carries forward the audit lessons from ch. 1 (Continuous Reading uses first-occurrence-then-familiar names; Verse-by-Verse reintroduces per verse).

### Naming (additions to the ch.1 table)
- Λευί → **Levi** (already a Hebrew name; no parenthetical needed). Ἁλφαῖος → **Chalfai (Alphaeus)**.
- The Twelve (3:16–19): Σίμων→**Shimon (Simon)**, renamed Πέτρος/Κηφᾶς→**Kefa (Peter)**; Ἀνδρέας→**Andreas (Andrew)**; Ἰάκωβος→**Ya'aqov (James)**; Ἰωάννης→**Yochanan (John)**; Βοανηργές→**Boanerges** with Mark's own in-text gloss "sons of thunder"; Φίλιππος→**Philippos (Philip)**; Βαρθολομαῖος→**Bar-Talmai (Bartholomew)**; Μαθθαῖος→**Mattai (Matthew)**; Θωμᾶς→**Toma (Thomas)**; Θαδδαῖος→**Taddai (Thaddaeus)**; Σίμων ὁ Καναναῖος→**Shimon the Kena'ani (Cananaean)** (from *qan'ana* "zealous", not "from Cana"); Ἰούδας Ἰσκαριώθ→**Yehudah Ish-Keriot (Judas Iscariot)** (*ish Keriot*).
- Ἡρῳδιανοί→**Herodians**; Βεελζεβούλ→**Beelzevul (Beelzebul)**; Τύρος→**Tsor (Tyre)**, Σιδών→**Tsidon (Sidon)**, Ἰδουμαία→**Idumea**; Ἀβιαθάρ→**Evyatar (Abiathar)**.

### Lexical / glossary
- **ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου → "the Son of Man"** (2:10, 2:28) — kept as the established rendering; Tier-2 note gives the literal "son of the human" and the Daniel 7:13 range (generic ↔ titular held together, not collapsed).
- **ἀφίημι (verb, 2:5,9) → "forgive"** in main text with the release sense noted — the verbal counterpart of *aphesis* "release" (1:4). Consistency flagged for Hellenist.
- **πνεῦμα 2:8 → "his spirit" (NO slash)** — Yeshua's own inner spirit; "wind" not a live sense (context-gated slash, same principle as "unclean spirit"). The holy-spirit slash is kept at 3:29.
- **κύριος 2:28 → "lord"** (not the divine name — not an OT quotation; "lord of the sabbath").
- **ἀμήν 3:28 → "truly / amen"**.

### Rule 30 (divine speech) scope for Mark 2–3
- **Marked:** Yeshua at 2:5, 2:8–11 (with the narrator aside at 2:10 breaking the span), 2:14, 2:17, 2:19–22, 2:25–28; 3:3, 3:4, 3:5, 3:23–29, 3:33–35.
- **NOT marked (logged):** the scribes' inner reasoning (2:6–7) and accusation (3:22); the crowd (2:12; 3:32); the fasting questioners (2:18,24); the family (3:21); and — importantly — the **unclean spirits' confession "You are the Son of God" (3:11)**, a non-divine speaker (parallel to 1:24).

### Text-critical variants pre-registered
- **2:26 "Evyatar (Abiathar) the high priest"** — names a different priest than 1 Sam 21 (Ahimelech); some witnesses (D, W) omit the phrase. Flagged 🔴 CRITICAL, not harmonized.
- **3:14 "[whom he also named emissaries/apostles]"** — bracketed in NA28 (possible import from Luke 6:13). Rendered in italic-brackets, flagged.
- **3:32 "[and your sisters]"** — present in some witnesses (A, D), absent in others (א, B, C, L). Included as text-uncertain (italics), flagged.

**Status:** provisional. Source-analysis corpus: `docs/source-analysis/greek/mark-2-3.md`.

## Entry 2026-06-18-003 — Mark PEOPLE + book CONTEXT (EN); canonical-home decisions

**PEOPLE.md canonical-home decisions (Rule 29 v3.3.2; stability over "most-substantive-arc" per the plan audit):**
- **See-only stubs → matthew/PEOPLE.md** for figures Matthew holds as full entries: **Yeshua (Jesus)**, **Yochanan the Immerser (John the Baptist)**, **Miryam (Mary)**. (Miryam appears in Mark 1–3 only as the unnamed "his mother," 3:31–35; named at 6:3.) No re-canonicalization to Mark, even though Mark is arguably a substantive arc for Yeshua — chose stability to avoid churn.
- **Full entries in Mark (first authored appearance):** Shimon Kefa (Simon Peter), Andreas (Andrew), Ya'aqov son of Zavdai (James), Yochanan son of Zavdai (John), Zavdai (Zebedee), Levi. These figures are not in Matthew 1–3 (the Twelve are called in Matt 4 / listed Matt 10), so no prior canonical entry exists.
- **Concise entries** for the rest of the Twelve named at 3:18–19 (Philippos, Bar-Talmai, Mattai, Toma, Ya'aqov son of Chalfai, Taddai, Shimon the Kena'ani, Yehudah Ish-Keriot) — named but with no narrative role in Mark 1–3.
- **Disambiguations logged:** two Yochanans (the Immerser vs. son of Zavdai); three Ya'aqovs (son of Zavdai / son of Chalfai / the patriarch); Levi (2:14) traditionally = the apostle Mattai (3:18, = Matthew 9:9), identification not stated by Mark; "Kena'ani" = *qan'ana* "zealous" (Zealot), not "from Cana"; "Ish-Keriot" = *ish Keriot* (man of Keriot).
