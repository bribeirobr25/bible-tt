## Entry 2026-06-17-001 — Mark activation + chapter 1 pilot (EN)

**Scope:** First book of the multi-book expansion (`docs/audit/MULTI_BOOK_EXPANSION_PLAN.md`). Mark 1 (EN) authored as the full-stack pilot; chapters 2–3, the other three locales, and the chapter companions follow.

**Base text:** NA28. Divine-name policy: GS Option C (*kyrios* → "the Lord" in OT-quotation contexts where the referent is YHWH; noted Tier 2).

### Naming decisions (RULES-GS proper-name convention; first occurrence "Transliterated (Familiar)", then familiar)
- Ἰησοῦς → **Yeshua (Jesus)**. Χριστός → **"the anointed one"** (lowercase, translated — matches Matthew precedent, *not* transliterated "Christ" or "Mashiach").
- Ἰωάννης → **Yochanan (John)** (the Immerser). Ἠσαΐας → **Yesha'yahu (Isaiah)**. Μωϋσῆς → **Moshe (Moses)**.
- Ἰορδάνης → **Yarden (Jordan)**. Ἰουδαία → **Yehudah (Judea)**. Ἱεροσόλυμα → **Yerushalayim (Jerusalem)**. Γαλιλαία → **Galil (Galilee)**. Ναζαρέτ → **Natzeret (Nazareth)**; the adjectival Ναζαρηνέ (1:24) → **Natzeri (of Nazareth)**. Καφαρναούμ → **Kfar Nachum (Capernaum)**.
- Σίμων → **Shimon (Simon)**. Ἀνδρέας → **Andreas (Andrew)**. Ἰάκωβος → **Ya'aqov (James)** (same name as the patriarch Jacob; familiar EN "James"). Ζεβεδαῖος → **Zavdai (Zebedee)**.

### Glossary / lexical decisions (locked-glossary alignment with Matthew/John)
- εὐαγγέλιον → **"good news"** (not "gospel"). βάπτισμα/βαπτίζω → **immersion/immerse** (not "baptism"). μετάνοια/μετανοέω → **change of mind / change *your* minds** (not "repentance"). κηρύσσω → **proclaim**.
- **βασιλεία τοῦ θεοῦ → "kingdom of God"** (1:15). NOTE: Mark uses "kingdom of God"; Matthew's distinctive "kingdom of the skies" (*tōn ouranōn*) does **not** appear in Mark. The TT renders each Gospel's actual phrase — no harmonizing.
- **πνεῦμα slash policy (Rule 2):** rendered **{a:wind/spirit}** for the divine spirit (1:8 holy spirit, 1:10 descent, 1:12 driving-out) — HB *ruach* alignment. For **πνεῦμα ἀκάθαρτον** ("unclean spirit", 1:23,26,27) the slash is **dropped** — "wind" is not a live sense in the demonic-possession context; rendered "unclean spirit". Logged as a deliberate context-gated exception to the blanket slash, consistent with Rule 2 (preserve *genuine* ambiguity only).
- **ἄφεσις ἁμαρτιῶν (1:4) → "release of sins"** in main text, with Tier-2 note that *aphesis* = release / sending-away / remission (concrete sense preserved over conventional "forgiveness"). Flagged for Hellenist confirmation.
- **σατανᾶς (1:13) → "the Satan"** (transliteration of Hebrew *ha-satan*, "the adversary"); Tier-2 note gives the adversary sense. Rule-3-sensitive (no imported demonology beyond the text); rendered as the text's term without elaboration.
- **λεπρός / λέπρα (1:40,42) → "leper" / "leprosy"** in main text, with Tier-2 note that Greek *lepra* (= HB *tsara'at*) is a broader category of skin/surface affliction, **not** modern Hansen's disease. Flagged.
- **εὐθύς ("immediately") preserved at every occurrence** (1:10,12,18,20,21,23,28,29,30,42,43) — Mark's signature adverb; no stylistic variation.

### Rule 30 (divine speech, `@@…@@`) scope for Mark 1
- **Marked:** God's voice 1:11; Yeshua's speech 1:15, 1:17, 1:25, 1:38, 1:41, 1:44.
- **NOT marked (logged):** the narrator's composite OT citation 1:2–3 (narrator quotation, incl. *kyrios*); the **unclean spirit's** speech 1:24 ("I know who you are — the holy one of God") — a non-divine speaker *about* Yeshua; the crowd 1:27; the disciples 1:37; the leper 1:40.
- **Mark 1:11 is second person** — @@"You are my son…"@@ — contrast Matthew 3:17's third-person "This is my son…". The TT preserves Mark's direct address; no harmonizing to Matthew.

### Text-critical variants pre-registered (RULES-GS variant-significance threshold)
- **1:1 [υἱοῦ θεοῦ] "son of God"** — bracketed/disputed in NA28 (absent from א*, Θ, 28 a.o.; present in א¹, B, D, L, W). Retained in main text (NA28 prints it), flagged 🔴 CRITICAL with the witness split.
- **1:2 "ἐν τῷ Ἠσαΐᾳ τῷ προφήτῃ" ("in Isaiah the prophet")** — NA28 reading; Byzantine reads "in the prophets" (ἐν τοῖς προφήταις), a harmonizing fix because the citation is **composite** (Malachi 3:1 / Exod 23:20 + Isaiah 40:3). Flagged 🔴 CRITICAL; the composite source is the reason for the variant.
- **1:41 σπλαγχνισθείς ("moved with compassion") vs ὀργισθείς ("moved with anger")** — NA28 reads "compassion"; the "anger" reading (Codex Bezae D + Old Latin) is the *lectio difficilior* and is text-critically significant. Flagged 🔴 CRITICAL with both readings.
