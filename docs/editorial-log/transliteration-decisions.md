# Transliteration Decision Log

**Purpose:** Record why each source-language term (Hebrew Bible and Greek Scriptures) crossed or did not cross the Rule 4 transliteration threshold. In the text, a transliterated strategic term is marked `{t:…}` (rendered teal + bold; RULES-CORE Text-Highlight Markers).
**Rule 4 criterion:** Transliterate when (1) no accurate target-language equivalent exists, (2) common translation carries misleading baggage, or (3) the term is structurally/cosmologically critical.
**Scope (v3.2 clarification):** This log governs **technical terms** (raqia, ish/ishah, nephilim, tebah, etc.) — not proper names. Proper names (persons, places, groups) are governed by the v3.2 Name Rendering Policy in RULES-CORE.md, with familiar-form tables in RULES-HB.md and RULES-GS.md.
**Ruleset version in force:** v3.4.1
**Created:** 2026-04-17 (v2.4.2) · **Last updated:** 2026-11-20 (Launch Program Step 4, batch 2: added the Genesis 6 terms and the Greek Scriptures section; moved *Chava* out of scope)
**Source of each entry:** this file records decisions already taken in the book editorial logs and the locked glossaries; it does not make new ones. Each entry cites its source. The full `{t:}` ↔ glossary/log reconciliation (sweep check S21) is a later batch.

## Transliterated (crossed threshold)

### רָקִיעַ → *raqia*
- **Threshold crossed:** Criterion 2 (misleading baggage) + Criterion 3 (cosmologically critical).
- **Why not "firmament":** Medieval Latin *firmamentum* imports solid-dome cosmology not explicit in Hebrew.
- **Why not "expanse":** Neutral but loses the root sense (ר-ק-ע = "to hammer/beat") that the note system is built to explain.
- **Policy:** Transliterated throughout Genesis 1 in Transparent Edition (all languages). Reader Edition may substitute "expanse" but must preserve "raqia" in notes.
- **First occurrence:** Gen 1:6.

### אִישׁ / אִשָּׁה → *ish / ishah* (at Gen 2:23 only)
- **Threshold crossed:** Criterion 1 (no equivalent preserving wordplay).
- **Why transliterate:** The phonetic pair *ish/ishah* is the entire point of Gen 2:23. English "man/woman" does not sound related. Transliteration makes the Hebrew wordplay visible.
- **Policy:** Transliterated at Gen 2:23 with parenthetical gloss; all other occurrences render as "man/woman" normally.
- **First occurrence:** Gen 2:23.

### נְפִילִים → *nephilim*
- **Threshold crossed:** Criterion 1 (no accurate equivalent) + Criterion 2 ("giants" imports LXX *gigantes*).
- **Policy:** Transliterated in all languages (EN/PT/ES *nephilim*, DE *Nephilim*); in the locked glossary.
- **First occurrence:** Gen 6:4.
- **Source:** `genesis.md` Entry 2026-04-21-082; RULES-HB locked glossary (נְפִילִים row).

### תֵּבָה → *tebah*
- **Threshold crossed:** Rule 4 (`genesis.md` Entry 2026-04-21-088 invokes Rule 4 without naming a criterion). The RULES-HB row reads: "Transliterate at first occurrence with gloss. NOT 'boat/barco/Boot/bote.'" No further reason is recorded in either source.
- **Policy:** Transliterated with a gloss at first occurrence (EN *tebah* (ark/vessel), PT *tebah* (arca), DE *Tebah* (Arche), ES *tebah* (arca)); "the tebah" thereafter in the Transparent Edition. Chapter overviews use the plain gloss (Pattern C de-jargon).
- **First occurrence:** Gen 6:14.
- **Source:** `genesis.md` Entry 2026-04-21-088; RULES-HB locked glossary (תֵּבָה row).

### גֹּפֶר → *gopher*
- **Threshold crossed:** Criterion 1 (the wood species is unknown; "cypress" is an unsupported guess).
- **Policy:** EN *gopher*, PT/ES *gofer*, DE *Gofer*. The *gopher*/*kopher* (pitch; cover/atone) wordplay at 6:14 is noted per Rule 14.
- **First occurrence:** Gen 6:14.
- **Source:** `genesis.md` Entry 2026-04-21-088; RULES-HB locked glossary (גֹּפֶר row).

### Moved out of scope: חַוָּה → *Chava*
- **Status:** `superseded` (2026-11-20). Reason: *Chava* is a proper name, and names are governed by the Name Rendering Policy (RULES-CORE v3.2+), not by Rule 4 — see the Scope line at the top of this file. The current rendering is "Chava (Eve)" at first occurrence per section, then the familiar form (RULES-HB proper-name table; `genesis.md` Entry 2026-04-16-039 for the original decision). The *chay* ("living") wordplay is explained in the Gen 3:20 note.
- The original entry (2026-04-17, v2.4.2) is kept below verbatim as history:
  - **Threshold crossed:** Criterion 2 (traditional "Eve" drifted far from Hebrew via Greek *Εὔα* → Latin *Eva*).
  - **Why transliterate:** Preserves wordplay with חַי (*chay*, "living") visible in the name. "Eve" obscures this connection entirely.
  - **Policy:** Transparent Edition: *Chava*. Reader Edition: may use "Eve" with note.
  - **First occurrence:** Gen 3:20.

## Not transliterated (did not cross threshold)

### נָחָשׁ → "serpent" (not *nachash*)
- **Threshold not crossed:** "Serpent" is a clean, direct English rendering.
- **Why not transliterate:** Unlike *raqia* (where every English equivalent carries cosmological baggage), "serpent" is semantically accurate without importing false connotation. The theological overlay (serpent = Satan) is a later reader's import, not a translation problem — it is addressed in the note, not by changing the rendering.
- **Consideration:** If future chapters reveal that *nachash* carries untranslatable connotation (e.g., wordplay with *nachash* = "divination/enchantment" at other occurrences), the decision may be revisited.

### תְהוֹם → "the deep" (not *tehom*)
- **Threshold not crossed:** "The deep" is functionally adequate.
- **Why not transliterate:** The term is not subject to the same cosmological baggage as *raqia*. "The deep" preserves the watery-abyss sense without over-specifying (Rule 12). The possible connection to Akkadian *Tiamat* is noted in Tier 2/3, not in the rendering.

### הִנֵּה → "see" (not *hinneh*)
- **Threshold not crossed:** "See" is a functional English deictic attention-marker.
- **Why not transliterate:** The term is pragmatically equivalent. No false connotation imported. Transliteration would add a stumbling-block without transparency gain.
- **Policy:** Locked in glossary (v2.4.2) as EN "see", PT "eis", DE "siehe".

### תּוֹלְדוֹת → "generations" (not *toledot*)
- **Threshold not crossed in main text:** "Generations" captures the core sense.
- **Why noted but not transliterated:** The root (י-ל-ד = "to beget") and structural-marker function are explained in the note. A transliteration in main text would interrupt narrative flow without proportional gain. Transliteration appears in the note as *toledot* for reference.

### אֵד → "mist" (not *ed*)
- **Threshold not crossed in main text:** "Mist" is the dominant traditional rendering.
- **Why noted:** The word is genuinely UNCERTAIN (only 2 occurrences in Hebrew Bible). The uncertainty is flagged in the note with alternatives (stream, flow, vapor). Transliterating *ed* in the main text would not help the reader since the Hebrew itself is obscure.

### מַבּוּל → "flood" (not *mabbul*)
- **Threshold not crossed:** the locked glossary renders it flood / dilúvio / Flut / diluvio, with a note on its near-unique distribution (Gen 6–11, Ps 29:10).
- **Source:** RULES-HB locked glossary (מַבּוּל row). (RULES-GS's transliteration note listed *mabbul* among the transliterated HB terms until v3.4.1; RULES-GS:48 now says *mabbul* is rendered "flood", "corrected in v3.4.1 to match the RULES-HB glossary".)

## Greek Scriptures

RULES-GS (Transliteration note under the locked glossary): no GS glossary term crosses the Rule 4 threshold, because Greek terms have clear semantic content. The entries below are the logged exceptions and the logged refusals.

### Transliterated

#### πάσχα → *Pesach*
- **Basis:** governed exception via HB→GS cross-supplement alignment (Rule 16): πάσχα is the Greek form of פֶּסַח; PT *Páscoa* / ES *Pascua* can mean Easter (Rule 3 anachronism risk).
- **Policy:** "Pesach" in all four languages; a note at first occurrence explains Greek πάσχα and the Hebrew/Aramaic background. Not a general rule that Greek terms are rendered through Hebrew.
- **First occurrence:** John 2:13.
- **Source:** `john.md` Entry J-012; RULES-GS locked glossary (πάσχα row).

#### οἱ Ἰουδαῖοι → *the Yehudim* (DE *die Jehudim*)
- **Basis:** "the Jews" carries reception-history baggage; "the Judeans" over-resolves to the geographic sense.
- **Policy:** EN "the Yehudim", PT "os Yehudim", DE "die Jehudim" (yod → J, as JHWH), ES "los Yehudim"; a Tier 2 note at each occurrence names the active sense (authorities / people / geographic-ethnic); anti-misuse note at first occurrence per book. This is a group term listed as an exception to the familiar-name default in the Name Rendering Policy.
- **First occurrence:** John 1:19.
- **Source:** `john.md` Entry J-006; RULES-GS §Ioudaioi Policy.

#### Ἁκελδαμάχ → *Hakeldama* (Field of Blood)
- **Basis:** an Aramaic place-term the text glosses itself ("that is, Field of Blood").
- **Policy:** `{t:Hakeldama}` plus the author's own gloss.
- **First occurrence:** Acts 1:19.
- **Source:** `acts.md` §Proper-name rendering (Ἁκελδαμάχ row).

#### ᾅδης → *Hades*
- **Policy:** `{t:Hades}` with a Sheol gloss (the LXX renders Sheol as Hades; Acts 2:27, 31 quote Ps 16).
- **First occurrence:** Acts 2:27.
- **Source:** `acts.md` §Strategic / locked terms.

#### σατανᾶς → "the Satan"
- **Policy:** rendered as the transliteration of Hebrew *ha-satan* ("the adversary"), with a Tier 2 note giving the adversary sense; no imported demonology (Rule 3).
- **First occurrence:** Mark 1:13.
- **Source:** `mark.md` §Glossary / lexical decisions.

### Not transliterated

#### λόγος → "word" (not *logos*)
- **Threshold not crossed:** λόγος has clear semantic content ("word/reason/account"). Lowercase "word / palavra / Wort / palabra"; capitalizing would import the Johannine referent into typography.
- **Source:** `john.md` Entry J-001.

#### Χριστός → "the anointed one" (not *Christ* or *Mashiach*)
- **Policy:** translated, lowercase "the anointed one" (RULES-GS locked glossary; capitalized only as a proper name; Rule 20). Launch decision L7 (2026-11-20) confirmed the lock over the "Messiah" renderings logged for Acts and 1 Peter; the content follows in a later batch. Μεσσίας (John 1:41) is a different word and is not covered here.
- **Source:** `mark.md` §Naming decisions (Matthew precedent); RULES-GS locked glossary; `docs/audit/CONTENT_SWEEP_FINDINGS.md` §3 (L7).
