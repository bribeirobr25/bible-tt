- *Words in italics* — added for English grammar (not in the Hebrew text)
- {t:raqia} — transliterated Hebrew terms kept untranslated (explained in notes)
- {a:wind/spirit} — a word the Hebrew leaves open to two meanings, both kept
- @@"…"@@ — direct speech of God

## CHAPTER OVERVIEW

**What happens:** God creates the ordered world in six days. Creation proceeds through divine speech ("and God said"), separation (light/dark, waters above/below, sea/land), population (vegetation, luminaries, creatures, humans), and blessing (fertility, dominion).

**Key themes:** Order from chaos; creation by speech; the expanse (sky) as cosmic structure; reproduction "to its kind"; humanity in the image of God; vegetarian diet assigned.

**Watch for:** The refrain "and God saw that good" (6×, then "very good"); the shift from the generic "God" to the personal name YHWH, which does not appear until Chapter 2; the plural "let us make" (1:26); dominion over creatures but not over waters as cosmic domain.

**Connection:** This chapter establishes the ordered world that Chapter 2 will revisit from a different angle, and that Chapters 6–9 will de-create and re-create through the flood.

## VERSE-BY-VERSE STUDY

### **Verse 1**

In beginning, God created the skies and the land.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - Structure & Interpretation**  
> Hebrew: **בְּרֵאשִׁית בָּרָא אֱלֹהִים אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָֽרֶץ** (*Bereshit bara elohim et hashamayim ve'et ha'aretz*)
> 
> - Seven-word structure in Hebrew (symbolic number) lost in translation
> - **בְּרֵאשִׁית** (*bereshit*) = "in beginning" (NO article—not "in THE beginning")
> - Grammatical structure permits TWO readings: (1) v.1 as independent sentence (traditional, followed here per Masoretic punctuation), or (2) v.1 as title/superscription with narrative beginning v.2. **POSSIBLE** that v.1 functions as chapter heading.
> 
> 🟢 **KEY TERMS**
> - **אֵת** (*et*) appears 2x—untranslatable direct object marker unique to Hebrew
> - **בָּרָא** (*bara*) = specific divine creation term used distinctively for divine creative action; its precise scope (whether it implies creation from nothing or reshaping existing material) is debated (**POSSIBLE**); distinct from **עָשָׂה** (*asah* = make/form)
> - **שָׁמַיִם** (*shamayim*) = physical sky (plural); NOT spiritual "Heaven"
> - **אֶרֶץ** (*eretz*) = land/ground; NOT planet "Earth"

---

### **Verse 2**

And the land was chaos and void, and darkness *was* over the face of the deep, and {a:wind/spirit} of God *was* hovering over the face of the waters.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟡 **AMBIGUITY PRESERVED**
> - **תֹהוּ וָבֹהוּ** (*tohu vavohu*) = rare phrase (appears only 3x in Bible); exact meaning **UNCERTAIN**; associated with destruction/desolation. "Chaos and void" is **POSSIBLE** translation. Avoid elaborate cosmological speculation.
> - **רוּחַ אֱלֹהִים** (*ruach elohim*) = wind/spirit/breath of God—ALL THREE meanings present; grammatically feminine in Hebrew
> 
> 🟢 **KEY TERMS**
> - **תְהוֹם** (*tehom*) = the deep/massive body of water; **PROBABLE** translation; avoid "cosmic ocean" (false precision)
> - **מְרַחֶפֶת** (*merachefet*) = present participle (ongoing action)—NOT simple past "hovered"; possibly "brooding" or "covering"

---

### **Verse 3**

And God said, @@"Shall be light,"@@ and light was.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **JUSSIVE & CONVERTING VAV** (Explained here; not repeated in later verses)
> - **יְהִי** (*yehi*) = future/jussive form used as divine command—literally "shall be" rather than imperative "let there be"
> - **Converting-vav (ו)** on **וַיְהִי** (*vayehi*) changes future "shall be" → past narrative "and was"—unique biblical Hebrew feature that propels narrative forward. This pattern continues throughout Genesis 1.

---

### **Verse 4**

And God saw the light, that good, and God separated between the light and between the darkness.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **COMPRESSED EVALUATION**
> - Hebrew **כִּי־טוֹב** (*ki-tov*) = "that good" without explicit "it was"—Transparent Edition preserves Hebrew compression; Reader Edition would expand to "that it was good"
> - "Between...and between" (**בֵּין...וּבֵין**) = Hebrew repetitive pattern preserved; continues in vv.6, 7, 14, 18

---

### **Verse 5**

And God called to the light "Day," and to the darkness he called "Night." And was evening, and was morning, one day.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - Day ONE, Not FIRST**
> - Hebrew **יוֹם אֶחָד** (*yom echad*) = "day ONE" (cardinal number)—NOT ordinal **רִאשׁוֹן** (*rishon* = "first"). Days 2-6 use ordinals (second, third, fourth, fifth, sixth). **Day 1 treated differently linguistically—this is significant.**
> 
> 🔵 **DAY CLOSURE FORMULA**
> - "And was evening, and was morning, [X] day" = LOCKED formula; repeats exactly in vv.8, 13, 19, 23, 31
> - Evening-morning order reflects Hebrew day beginning at sunset

---

### **Verse 6**

And God said, @@"Shall be *a* {t:raqia} (expanse) within the waters, and *it* shall be a separator between waters to waters."@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - RAQIA INTRODUCTION**
> - Hebrew **רָקִיעַ** (*raqia*) from root **ר-ק-ע** = "to hammer/beat"—describes hammered or beaten-out structure
> - **TRANSLITERATED** throughout Genesis 1 to prevent false cosmological precision
> - Verse 8 will identify **raqia = shamayim (sky)**
> 
> 🔵 **STRUCTURE**
> - **בְּתוֹךְ** (*betoch*) = "within/inside" the waters (the raqia forms inside the water mass)—not "amid/among"
> - **מַבְדִּיל** (*mavdil*) = Hiphil participle used substantivally: "a separator/divider"—not the finite "separates" or the infinitive "to separate"

---

### **Verse 7**

And God made the {t:raqia}, and he separated between the waters which *were* under the {t:raqia} and between the waters which *were* above the {t:raqia}. And it was so.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **VERB SHIFT**
> - **בָּרָא** (*bara* = created, v.1) vs. **עָשָׂה** (*asah* = made, v.7)—both used for divine action; **POSSIBLE** distinction: *bara* is used distinctively for divine creative action; its precise scope (whether it implies creation from nothing or reshaping existing material) is debated
> 
> 🔵 **FORMULA INTRODUCED**
> - "And it was so" (**וַיְהִי־כֵן**) appears for first time; repeats in vv.9, 11, 15, 24, 30

---

### **Verse 8**

And God called to the {t:raqia} "Sky." And was evening, and was morning, second day.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - RAQIA = SKY IDENTIFICATION**
> - God explicitly identifies **raqia = shamayim (sky)**—the text equates raqia with the visible sky/expanse above. The semantic range of raqia (from root "to hammer/beat out") remains debated; ancient interpreters understood it variously as a solid vault, an atmospheric expanse, or a boundary layer. The naming here grounds the term in the sky domain without resolving those questions.
> - Subsequent verses using "raqia" (vv.14-20) maintain this identification
> 
> 🔵 **DAY 2 ORDINAL**
> - **יוֹם שֵׁנִי** (*yom sheni*) = "second day" (ordinal)—unlike Day 1's cardinal "one"

---

### **Verse 9**

And God said, @@"Shall be gathered the waters from under the sky to one place, and shall be seen the dry ground."@@ And it was so.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **PASSIVE VOICE PRESERVED**
> - **יִקָּווּ** (*yikavu*) = "shall be gathered" (passive); **תֵרָאֶה** (*teira'eh*) = "shall be seen" (passive)—not active commands to waters, but passive divine causation
> 
> 🟢 **WORD ROOT**
> - **יִקָּווּ** from root **ק-ו-ה** (gather/collect)—related to **מִקְוֵה** (*miqveh* = ritual bath, water-gathering)
> - Singular "place" for land (connected landmass) anticipates plural "seas" v.10

---

### **Verse 10**

And God called to the dry ground "Land," and to the gathering of the waters he called "Seas." And God saw that good.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **SINGULAR vs. PLURAL**
> - **אֶרֶץ** (*eretz*) = land (SINGULAR connected landmass); **יַמִּים** (*yamim*) = seas (PLURAL separated bodies of water)
> - Hebrew distinguishes one land from multiple seas (Mediterranean + Red Sea separated in ancient geography)

---

### **Verse 11**

And God said, @@"Shall grass the land grass, herbs seeding seed, fruit trees making fruit to its kind, which its seed *is* in it, upon the land."@@ And it was so.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **RARE VERB - "TO GRASS"**
> - **תַּדְשֵׁא** (*tadsheh*) = verb created from noun **דֶּשֶׁא** (grass)—similar to English "to grass" (produce grass). YES, "shall grass" sounds odd—**intentional preservation of Hebrew poetic style.**
> 
> 🔴 **ROOT DOUBLING PATTERNS** (Hebrew poetic device)
> - **מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע** (*mazria zera*) = "seeding seed"; **עֹשֶׂה פְרִי** (*oseh pri*) = "making fruit"
> - Shows internal production/generation—**preserved despite awkwardness**
> - **דֶּשֶׁא עֵשֶׂב** (*deshe esev*) = "grass, herbs" (synonym pairing for stylistic effect)
> 
> 🟢 **REPRODUCTION PATTERN**
> - **לְמִינוֹ** (*lemino*) = "to its kind/type"—describes reproduction pattern

---

### **Verse 12**

And the land brought out grass, herbs seeding seed to its kind, and trees making fruit which its seed *is* in it, to its kind. And God saw that good.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **COMMAND → FULFILLMENT PATTERN**
> - v.11 command → v.12 fulfillment with nearly identical wording—shows exact compliance
> - Root doubling maintained: "seeding seed," "making fruit"

### **Verse 14**

And God said, @@"Shall be lights in the {t:raqia}, to separate between the day and between the night, and they shall be for signs and for appointed times and for days and years.@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **LUMINARIES INTRODUCED**
> - **מְאֹרֹת** (*me'orot*) = lights/luminaries/light-bearers from root **א-ו-ר** (light)—plural but specific referents unnamed until v.16

> 🔴 **RAQIA REFERENCE**
> - After v.8 identified raqia = sky, this verse places lights "in the raqia"
> 
> 🟢 **APPOINTED TIMES**
> - **מוֹעֲדִים** (*mo'adim*) = appointed times/seasons/festivals—can mean natural seasons OR sacred calendar events; meaning range preserved

### **Verse 16**

And God made the two great lights: the greater light for dominion of the day, and the lesser light for dominion of the night, and the stars.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - UNNAMED LUMINARIES**
> - Sun and moon **LEFT UNNAMED**—described only as "greater light" and "lesser light"
> - It has been noted that Genesis 1:16 avoids the common Hebrew words for sun (*shemesh*) and moon (*yareach*), which are also names of deities in the ancient Near East. Whether this avoidance is deliberate is debated (**POSSIBLE**).
> - Transparent Edition resists identifying them by name
> 
> 🟢 **DOMINION = TEMPORAL RULE**
> - **מֶמְשֶׁלֶת** (*memsheleth*) = dominion/rule over **time periods**—NOT territorial rule
> - "And the stars" added almost as syntactic afterthought—structure emphasizes the two lights
> - **עָשָׂה** (*asah* = made) used, NOT **בָּרָא** (*bara* = created)

### **Verse 20**

And God said, @@"Shall swarm the waters *with* swarms of living beings, and birds shall fly over the land, over the face of the {t:raqia}."@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - LIVING BEINGS INTRODUCED**
> - **נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה** (*nefesh chayah*) = living being/beings—literally "living breath/throat/soul"; **NOT "soul" in later philosophical sense**
> - Added to glossary; used in vv.21, 24, 30
> 
> 🟢 **ROOT DOUBLING**
> - **שֶׁרֶץ** (*sherets* = swarms) from **יִשְׁרְצוּ** (*yishretzu* = shall swarm)
> - **יְעוֹפֵף** (*ye'ofef*) = doubled/intensive form "flying-fly"
> 
> 🔴 **SPATIAL RELATIONSHIP**
> - Birds fly **OVER the face of** the raqia—NOT **IN** it (contrast: lights **IN** raqia)

### **Verse 21**

And God created the great sea creatures, and every living being, the moving *ones* which the waters swarmed, to their kinds, and every winged bird to its kind. And God saw that good.

> **📝 NOTES**

> 🔴 **CRITICAL - CREATED, NOT MADE**
> - **בָּרָא** (*bara* = created) returns for first time since v.1—used for living beings, NOT for lights or plants; suggests significance distinction
> 
> 🟢 **SEA CREATURES**
> - **תַּנִּינִים** (*taninim*) = great sea creatures/large aquatic animals—avoiding "sea monsters" or "dragons" (mythological overlay)
> - **לְמִינֵהֶם/לְמִינֵהוּ** (*leminehem/leminehu*) = "to their kinds"—reproduction pattern as in v.11

### **Verse 22**

And God blessed them, saying, @@"Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the waters in the seas, and *let* the birds multiply in the land."@@

> 🔴 **FIRST BLESSING FORMULA**
> - **וַיְבָרֶךְ** (*vayvarekh*) = "and he blessed" (first occurrence)—will recur: v.28 (humans), Genesis 2:3 (seventh day)

> 🔵 **TRIPLE IMPERATIVE**
> - **פְּרוּ** (*peru*) = be fruitful; **וּרְבוּ** (*urvu*) = multiply; **וּמִלְאוּ** (*umil'u*) = fill
> - **לֵאמֹר** (*lemor* = "saying") introduces direct divine speech to creatures—unusual narrative feature

### **Verse 24**

And God said, @@"Shall bring forth the land living beings to their kind: livestock and creeping things and living things of *the* land, to their kind."@@ And it was so.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **NEFESH CHAYAH - Land Animals**
> - **נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה** now applied to land animals (previously water creatures, vv.20-21)
> 
> 🔵 **THREE CATEGORIES**
> - **בְּהֵמָה** (*behemah*) = livestock/cattle; **רֶמֶשׂ** (*remes*) = creeping things; **חַיְתוֹ־אֶרֶץ** (*chayeto-eretz*) = living things of land/wild animals
> - Pattern parallels Day 3 vegetation: land brings forth on divine command

### **Verse 25**

And God made the living things of the land to their kind, and the livestock to their kind, and every creeping thing of the ground to its kind. And God saw that good.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **GROUND vs. LAND**
> - **אֲדָמָה** (*adamah*) = ground/soil (FIRST occurrence)—distinct from **אֶרֶץ** (*eretz* = land)
> - **עָשָׂה** (*asah* = made), NOT **בָּרָא** (*bara* = created)
> - Command (v.24) → fulfillment (v.25) pattern as in Days 3-4

### **Verse 26**

And God said, @@"Let us make *a* human in our image, according to our likeness, and let them have dominion over *the* fish of the sea and over *the* birds of the sky and over the livestock and over all the land and over every creeping thing *that* creeps upon the land."@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟡 **CRITICAL - PLURAL DIVINE SPEECH**
> - First person **PLURAL**: "let **us** make...in **our** image...our likeness"
> - **POSSIBLE** interpretations: (1) royal plural, (2) divine council, (3) literary plural
> - Note marks **UNCERTAINTY**—does NOT impose Trinity interpretation
> 
> 🟢 **HUMAN = GENERIC**
> - **אָדָם** (*adam*) WITHOUT article = generic human/humanity—NOT yet proper name "Adam"
> 
> 🔴 **IMAGE & LIKENESS**
> - **צֶלֶם** (*tselem*) = image; **דְּמוּת** (*demut*) = likeness
> - Both = representation/resemblance; paired for emphasis, NOT identical meaning
> - **Cross-reference:** The pair re-appears at **Gen 5:3** — Adam begets Seth "in his likeness, according to his image," inverting the divine-human order into human-human descent. For fuller discussion, see companion Section A12b.
> 
> 🟢 **DOMINION = FUNCTIONAL RULE**
> - **רָדָה** (*radah*) = have dominion/rule/govern—functional authority, **NOT ownership**
> - Verb shifts: "make A human" (singular) → "let THEM have dominion" (plural)
> - **הָרֶמֶשׂ הָֽרֹמֵשׂ** (*haremes haromes*) = "creeping thing that creeps" (root doubling)

---

### **Verse 27**

And God created the human in his image; in *the* image of God he created him; male and female he created them.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - TRIPLE CREATION EMPHASIS**
> - **בָּרָא** (*bara* = created) used **THREE TIMES**—**ONLY occurrence** of triple verb repetition in one verse; shows maximum significance/emphasis
> 
> 🔵 **POETIC STRUCTURE**
> - Three parallel clauses with **bara** anchoring each—elevated literary style
> - Singular/plural shift: "the human...him" (SINGULAR) → "male and female...them" (PLURAL)—collective humanity becomes differentiated
> 
> 🟢 **BIOLOGICAL DIFFERENTIATION**
> - **זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה** (*zakhar unqevah*) = male and female—grammatical terms for biological sex, NOT gendered cultural roles
> - **הָאָדָם** (*ha'adam*) = "THE human" (with article) vs. v.26 **אָדָם** (without article)—**POSSIBLE** emphasis

---

### **Verse 28**

And God blessed them, and God said to them, @@"Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the land and subdue it, and have dominion over *the* fish of the sea and over *the* birds of the sky and over every living thing *that* moves upon the land."@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **BLESSING FORMULA**
> - As v.22 (sea creatures/birds), but expanded for humans with additional commands
> 
> 🔴 **FIVE IMPERATIVES**
> - Be fruitful, multiply, fill, **subdue**, **have dominion**
> 
> 🟢 **SUBDUE = CULTIVATION**
> - **כָּבַשׁ** (*kabash*) = subdue/bring under cultivation—agricultural term (appears ONLY here in Genesis 1); **NOT license for exploitation or violence**
> - **רָדָה** (*radah*) now as imperative—functional rule over animals, NOT absolute ownership
>
> 🔵 **CROSS-REFERENCE: POST-FLOOD REPETITION**
> - The "be fruitful and multiply and fill the land" command re-appears verbatim at **Gen 9:1** — the creation-mandate renewed after the flood. The dominion language in 9:2 shifts tone (animals *fear* humans) and diet permission expands (9:3, meat permitted). For fuller discussion, see companion Section G5b.

### **Verse 29**

And God said, @@"See, I have given to you every herb seeding seed which *is* upon *the* face of all the land, and every tree in which *is* fruit of *a* tree seeding seed; to you it shall be for food.@@

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔵 **ATTENTION PARTICLE**
> - **הִנֵּה** (*hineh*) = "see/behold"—signals important announcement
> 
> 🟢 **ROOT DOUBLING**
> - **זֹרֵעַ זֶרַע** (*zorea zera*) = "seeding seed"—pattern from vv.11-12
> 
> 🟡 **ORIGINAL DIET**
> - Vegetation ONLY for humans—NO animal consumption permitted yet (contrast Genesis 9:3 post-flood permission)

### **Verse 30**

@@And to every living thing of the land and to every bird of the sky and to everything creeping upon the land, in which *is a* living being, every green herb *shall be* for food."@@ And it was so.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🟢 **NEFESH CHAYAH = Qualifier**
> - **נֶפֶשׁ חַיָּה** distinguishes animals with breath/life from plants
> 
> 🟡 **UNIVERSAL VEGETARIAN DIET**
> - ALL living beings eat only plants initially—animals also vegetarian in original creation
> - **יֶרֶק עֵשֶׂב** (*yereq esev*) = green herb/fresh vegetation

### **Verse 31**

And God saw all that he had made, and see, *it was* very good. And was evening, and was morning, the sixth day.

> **📝 NOTES**
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - EVALUATION SHIFT: VERY GOOD**
> - **טוֹב מְאֹד** (*tov me'od*) = "VERY good" vs. simple **טוֹב** (*tov* = "good") in ALL previous days
> - **ONLY occurrence** of "very good" in creation account—shows comprehensive positive evaluation after human creation
> - **THIS IS NOT STYLISTIC VARIATION—IT'S THEOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE**
> 
> 🔵 **HINEH = Attention**
> - **הִנֵּה** (*hineh* = "see/behold") appears with evaluation—draws attention to comprehensive goodness
> - "All that he had made"—entire creation reviewed
> 
> 🔴 **CRITICAL - ARTICLE ON "THE SIXTH DAY"**
> - **יוֹם הַשִּׁשִּׁי** (*yom hashishi*) = "THE sixth day" with definite article **ה**
> - **UNIQUE among all six days** (Days 1-5 have NO article)
> - **POSSIBLE** significance: completion emphasis, human creation importance, or preparation for Sabbath (Genesis 2:1-3)
> - **Many translations miss this distinction**

## ROOT DOUBLING PATTERNS

Hebrew poetic device showing internal generation:
- v.11-12: "seeding seed" (**מַזְרִיעַ זֶרַע**), "making fruit" (**עֹשֶׂה פְרִי**)
- v.20: "swarms" from "shall swarm"
- v.26: "creeping thing that creeps" (**הָרֶמֶשׂ הָֽרֹמֵשׂ**)
- v.29: "seeding seed" (**זֹרֵעַ זֶרַע**)

**Preserved despite sounding awkward—intentional Hebrew poetic style.**

## VERB SHIFTS - CREATED vs. MADE

| Verse | Action | Verb | Hebrew |
|-------|--------|------|--------|
| v.1 | Skies and land | **created** | *bara* בָּרָא |
| v.7 | Raqia | made | *asah* עָשָׂה |
| v.16 | Lights | made | *asah* |
| v.21 | Sea creatures & living beings | **created** | *bara* |
| v.25 | Land animals | made | *asah* |
| v.27 | Human (3x emphasis) | **created** | *bara* |

**Pattern:** *Bara* (created) for initial creation, living beings, humans. *Asah* (made) for raqia, lights, land animals. **POSSIBLE** distinction: *bara* is used distinctively for divine creative action; its precise scope (whether it implies creation from nothing or reshaping existing material) is debated.
