## READING GUIDE

**📖 Main Text:** Primary translation—readable but faithful to Hebrew structure
**📝 Notes:** Essential Hebrew features immediately below each verse
***Italics:*** Words added for English grammar (not in Hebrew text)
**"Quotes":** Direct divine speech
**YHWH:** Tetragrammaton rendered consonantally (Rule 25, Option A)
**tebah:** Transliterated Hebrew term for ark/vessel (explained in notes). *Mabbul* is the Hebrew term rendered as "flood."

**Note Icons:**
- 🔴 **CRITICAL** - Major textual or translation decisions
- 🟢 **LEXICAL** - Word meanings, root patterns, glossary terms
- 🔵 **GRAMMATICAL** - Hebrew structure, verb forms, syntax
- 🟡 **THEOLOGICAL** - Interpretive options, ambiguity preservation

**This chapter narrates the entry into the tebah and the onset of the flood.** The tension between "two from all" (6:19) and "seven seven" of clean animals (7:2) is preserved without harmonization per Rule 22. The cosmological mechanism — fountains of the deep and windows of the sky — reverses the Day 2 separation of waters (Gen 1:6–7). Divine name distribution shifts between YHWH and Elohim within the chapter.

---

## CHAPTER OVERVIEW

**What happens:** YHWH commands Noah to enter the ark with his family and the animals — clean animals by sevens, others by twos. After seven days, the flood begins: fountains of the deep burst open and the windows of the sky open. Waters rise for 40 days, covering all high mountains. Every land-breathing creature outside the ark dies. Waters prevail for 150 days.

**Key themes:** Clean/unclean distinction predating Sinai; the flood as cosmological reversal — the waters above and below the expanse (Day 2) reunite; the flood (a Hebrew word used only for this event); specific calendar dates throughout; YHWH closes the door (7:16 — Noah does not seal himself in); total destruction of land life.

**Watch for:** "Seven seven" for clean animals (7:2) after "two from all" (6:19–20) — the TT preserves both without harmonization; the flood dates create a precise chronology spanning one solar year; "YHWH closed behind him" (7:16) — the divine name appears at the moment of sealing; the phrase "all the high mountains under all the skies" (7:19) — the text's own scope claim.

**Connection:** The flood mechanism (7:11) precisely reverses Genesis 1:6–7 (the expanse separating the waters). Chapter 8 will reverse the flood, re-establishing Day 2's separation.

## VERSE-BY-VERSE STUDY

### **Verse 1**

And YHWH said to Noach (Noah), "Come, you and all your household, into the tebah, for you I have seen *as* righteous before me in this generation.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔴 **DIVINE NAME — YHWH**
> - This section (7:1–5) uses **YHWH**, not Elohim. YHWH speaks the personal, intimate command. Contrast with 7:9,16a where the narrator uses "God" (Elohim) for the structural/cosmic framing. Textual feature preserved.
>
> 🟢 **"RIGHTEOUS" (*tsaddiq*)**
> - **צַדִּיק** (*tsaddiq*) — same word as 6:9. But 6:9 says "righteous... whole in his generations" (narrator's description). Here YHWH says "righteous before me in this generation" — divine confirmation, but without *tamim* (whole/blameless). Whether the omission is significant: POSSIBLE.

---

### **Verse 2**

From all the clean livestock you shall take for yourself seven seven, a man and his woman, and from the livestock which *is* not clean, two, a man and his woman.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "SEVEN SEVEN" vs. "TWO FROM ALL" (6:19)**
> - **שִׁבְעָה שִׁבְעָה** (*shiv'ah shiv'ah*) = "seven seven" — distributive repetition. Ambiguous: (1) seven individual animals of each clean kind; (2) seven pairs (fourteen total). The Hebrew does not say "seven pairs" — it says "seven seven." The TT preserves the literal repetition.
> - **Tension with Gen 6:19:** Gen 6:19 says "two from all." Gen 7:2 says "seven seven" of the clean. This is the most visible numerical tension in the flood narrative. Rule 22 + No-Harmonization Principle: both are translated as MT reads them.
>
> 🟢 **"A MAN AND HIS WOMAN" — SOCIAL-PAIR LANGUAGE**
> - **אִישׁ וְאִשְׁתּוֹ** (*ish ve-ishto*) = "a man and his woman" — social/relational pair language, used specifically for clean animals in v.2. Contrast with **זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה** (*zakhar u-neqevah*, "male and female") — biological-sex categories used for birds (v.3) and the general animal entry (vv.9, 16). The TT renders each phrase as MT reads it; the two idioms are not harmonized. For fuller discussion, see companion Section A9.
>
> 🟢 **"CLEAN" — BEFORE SINAI**
> - **הַטְּהוֹרָה** (*hat-tehorah*) = "the clean." The clean/unclean distinction appears here before the Levitical legislation (Lev 11). The text assumes the reader knows the distinction — or the distinction is presented as pre-Sinai.

---

### **Verse 3**

Also from the bird of the sky, seven seven, male and female, to keep alive seed on the face of all the land.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🟢 **"SEED" (*zera*)**
> - **זֶרַע** (*zera*) = seed/offspring. Preserving biological lineage. Same word as 3:15 ("seed of the woman"). The flood preserves the *zera* chain.

---

### **Verse 4**

For in yet seven days I *am* sending rain upon the land, forty days and forty nights, and I will wipe every standing thing which I made from upon the face of the ground."

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🟢 **"STANDING THING" (*yequm*)**
> - **הַיְקוּם** (*ha-yequm*) = "the standing thing" — everything that stands/exists. From root ק-ו-ם (*q-w-m*, to stand/rise). Rare word — occurs only here and at Deut 11:6.
>
> 🔵 **FORTY DAYS — PATTERN**
> - Forty days and nights: a recurring duration in the Hebrew Bible (Exod 24:18 Moses on Sinai; 1 Kgs 19:8 Elijah's journey). Whether "forty" is literal or typological, the text presents it as a specific period.

---

### **Verse 5**

And Noah did according to all that YHWH commanded him.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **OBEDIENCE FORMULA — SECOND OCCURRENCE**
> - Repeats the formula from 6:22. This is the YHWH version; 6:22 used "God" (Elohim). The formula will repeat at 7:9 and 7:16.

---

### **Verse 6**

And Noah *was* six hundred years old, and the flood of waters was upon the land.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **CHRONOLOGICAL PRECISION**
> - The flood narrative provides exact dates: Noah's age (600), month/day at 7:11. This precision continues through 8:4,5,13,14. The chronological framework is a structural feature of the text.

### **Verse 9**

two two they came to Noah into the tebah, male and female, as God commanded Noah.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔴 **"TWO TWO" — AFTER "SEVEN SEVEN"**
> - **שְׁנַיִם שְׁנַיִם** (*shenayim shenayim*) = "two two." After the "seven seven" instruction at 7:2, the execution at 7:9 says "two two." The TT preserves this without reconciliation. The textual tension is a feature of the MT.
>
> 🔵 **"GOD" (ELOHIM) — NAME SHIFT**
> - v.5: "YHWH commanded." v.9: "God (*Elohim*) commanded." The narrator alternates within the same passage. Preserved as textual feature.

---

### **Verse 10**

And it was at seven of the days, and the waters of the flood were upon the land.

---

### **Verse 11**

In the six hundredth year of the life of Noah, in the second month, on the seventeenth day of the month, on this day all the fountains of the great deep burst open, and the windows of the sky were opened.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — COSMOLOGICAL MECHANISM**
> - **מַעְיְנֹת תְּהוֹם רַבָּה** (*ma'yenot tehom rabbah*) = "fountains of the great deep." *Tehom* = the primordial deep from Gen 1:2. The subterranean waters burst upward.
> - **אֲרֻבֹּת הַשָּׁמַיִם** (*arubbot ha-shamayim*) = "windows of the sky." The sky has openings through which the waters above the raqia descend.
> - This **reverses Gen 1:6–7**: the raqia was placed to separate waters above from waters below. The flood **undoes Day 2** — the cosmic water-boundary is breached from both directions. De-creation.
>
> 🔵 **PRECISE DATE**
> - Second month, seventeenth day. The waters recede on the same calendar framework (8:4,5,13,14). The flood has a beginning and an end on the same timeline.

### **Verse 13**

On this very day Noah entered, and Shem and Ham and Japheth, the sons of Noah, and the woman of Noah and the three women of his sons with them, into the tebah —

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **"ON THIS VERY DAY" (*be-etsem ha-yom ha-zeh*)**
> - **בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה** = "on the bone/essence of this day" — emphatic temporal marker. Same phrase used for Abraham's circumcision (Gen 17:23,26) and Israel's departure from Egypt (Exod 12:41,51).

---

### **Verse 14**

they, and every living thing to its kind, and every livestock to its kind, and every creeping thing that creeps upon the land to its kind, and every bird to its kind, every winged thing.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **"TO ITS KIND" — CREATION ECHO**
> - **לְמִינָהּ / לְמִינֵהוּ** (*leminah / leminehu*) — same *lemin* formula from Gen 1:11,12,21,24,25. The creation categories structure the preservation. What was made "to its kind" is saved "to its kind."

### **Verse 15**

And they came to Noah into the tebah, two two from all the flesh in which *is* breath of life.

---

### **Verse 16**

And those coming, male and female from all flesh they came, as God commanded him; and YHWH shut *it* behind him.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — YHWH SHUTS THE TEBAH**
> - **וַיִּסְגֹּר יהוה בַּעֲדוֹ** (*vayyisgor YHWH ba'ado*) = "and YHWH shut behind him." God (*Elohim*) commands; YHWH performs the intimate, protective act of sealing the tebah. The divine name shifts mid-verse: "as God commanded" → "YHWH shut." The act of closing is personal.
>
> 🔵 **BOTH NAMES IN ONE VERSE**
> - First half: "God" (cosmic authority). Second half: "YHWH" (intimate, protective act). Both names in a single verse — the text does not resolve the distinction. Preserved as feature.

---

### **Verse 17**

And the flood was forty days upon the land, and the waters increased and lifted the tebah, and it rose from upon the land.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **"LIFTED" AND "ROSE"**
> - **וַיִּרְבּוּ הַמַּיִם וַיִּשְׂאוּ אֶת־הַתֵּבָה** = the waters "carried/lifted" the tebah, and it "rose high from upon the land." The tebah is passive — it does not navigate. It is lifted by the waters and carried.

---

### **Verse 18**

And the waters prevailed and increased greatly upon the land, and the tebah went upon the face of the waters.

---

### **Verse 19**

And the waters prevailed very very upon the land, and all the high mountains which *were* under all the sky were covered.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **"VERY VERY" (*me'od me'od*)**
> - **מְאֹד מְאֹד** (*me'od me'od*) — doubled intensifier. The same doubling appears at Gen 17:2,6,20; 30:43. Hebrew emphasis through repetition.

---

### **Verse 20**

Fifteen cubits upward the waters prevailed, and the mountains were covered.

---

### **Verse 21**

And all flesh that moves upon the land perished — the bird and the livestock and the living thing and every swarming thing that swarms upon the land, and every human.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **DESTRUCTION ORDER**
> - Bird → livestock → living thing → swarming thing → human. Echoes the creation categories of Gen 1. All that was made living upon the land perishes.

---

### **Verse 22**

Everything which *had* breath of the wind/spirit of life in its nostrils, from all which *was* on the dry ground, died.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🟢 **"BREATH OF THE SPIRIT OF LIFE"**
> - **נִשְׁמַת רוּחַ חַיִּים** (*nishmat ruach chayyim*) = "breath of the spirit/wind of life." Triple compound: *neshamah* (breath) + *ruach* (spirit/wind) + *chayyim* (life). Only here in this full form. Combines Gen 2:7 (*nishmat chayyim*, breath of life) with Gen 6:17 (*ruach chayyim*, spirit of life).
>
> 🟢 **"DRY GROUND" (*charabah*)**
> - **חָרָבָה** (*charabah*) = dry ground. Distinct from *eretz* (land) and *adamah* (ground/soil). Specifies land as opposed to water — everything on the dry ground, as opposed to what survives in water.

---

### **Verse 23**

And he wiped every standing thing which *was* on the face of the ground, from human to livestock to creeping thing to bird of the sky, and they were wiped from the land; and only Noah remained, and those with him in the tebah.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🟢 **"WIPED" (*machah*) — FULFILLMENT**
> - 6:7: "I will wipe (*emcheh*)"; 7:4: "I will wipe (*machah*)"; 7:23: "he wiped (*vayyimach*)." Promise → restatement → fulfillment. Same verb throughout.
>
> 🔵 **"ONLY NOAH REMAINED"**
> - **וַיִּשָּׁאֶר אַךְ־נֹחַ** = "and remained only Noach." The particle *akh* (only/surely) emphasizes isolation. The entire living world outside the tebah is gone.

---

### **Verse 24**

And the waters prevailed upon the land one hundred and fifty days.

> **📝 NOTES**
>
> 🔵 **150 DAYS — CHRONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK**
> - The 150 days of prevailing appears again at 8:3. The flood narrative is structured around precise time markers: 7 days (7:4,10), 40 days (7:12,17), 150 days (7:24, 8:3), then the recession timeline in Gen 8.

## CROSS-CHAPTER TRACKING (Gen 6 → Gen 7)

**Obedience formula chain:**
- 6:22: "Noah did according to all that God commanded him; so he did"
- 7:5: "Noah did according to all that YHWH commanded him"
- 7:9: "as God commanded Noah"
- 7:16: "as God commanded him"
- Note the divine name alternation within the formula: God (6:22) → YHWH (7:5) → God (7:9, 7:16a) → YHWH closes the ark (7:16b).

**"Two from all" tension:**
- 6:19: "two from all you shall bring"
- 7:2: "seven seven" of clean, "two" of unclean
- 7:9: "two two they came"
- 7:15: "two two from all flesh"
- The tension is internal to the text. Rule 22: translate as MT reads, without harmonization.

**Creation reversal continues:**
- Gen 1:6–7: raqia separates waters above from waters below
- Gen 7:11: "fountains of the great deep burst open, windows of the sky opened" — Day 2's separation undone
- Gen 1: "God saw that good" → Gen 7:23: "he wiped every standing thing"
- The *lemin* formula (to its kind) from Gen 1 appears in 7:14 — creation categories structure preservation

**De-creation mechanism:**
- The flood is not merely "a lot of rain." It is the structural reversal of the raqia separation (Gen 1:6–7). Waters above + waters below = the pre-creation state of Gen 1:2 (*tehom*). The flood is de-creation by water-reunion.

**"Breath of life" chain:**
- 2:7: God breathes *nishmat chayyim* (breath of life) into the human
- 6:17: all flesh with *ruach chayyim* (spirit of life) will die
- 7:15: "two two from all flesh in which is *ruach chayyim*"
- 7:22: "everything which had *nishmat ruach chayyim*" (breath of the wind/spirit of life) — the most expanded form, combining both earlier phrases

**Divine name distribution in Gen 7:**
- vv.1–5: YHWH (personal command to Noah)
- vv.7–10: no divine name (narrative)
- v.9: God/Elohim ("as God commanded")
- v.16a: God/Elohim ("as God commanded him")
- v.16b: YHWH ("YHWH shut behind him")
- vv.17–24: no divine name (description of the flood)
