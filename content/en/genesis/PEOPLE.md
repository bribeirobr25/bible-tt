## Adam

**Meaning:** "human" / "of the ground" (*adam* from *adamah*)
**Origin:** CREATED
**Birth year:** AM 1 (by definition)
**Death year:** AM 930 (Gen 5:5)
**Lifespan:** 930 years (Gen 5:5)
**Father:** none (formed by YHWH Elohim from dust of the ground, Gen 2:7)
**Mother:** none
**Spouse(s):** Chava (Eve)
**Children:** Qayin (Cain), Hevel (Abel), Shet (Seth); "sons and daughters" (Gen 5:4)
**Location(s):** Garden of Eden; expelled eastward (Gen 3:24); the ground (*adamah*)
**First mention:** Gen 1:26–27 (as "human"); Gen 2:7 (formed); Gen 5:1 (as proper name "Adam")
**Mentioned in:** Gen 1:26–27; 2:7–25; 3:1–24; 4:1, 25; 5:1–5
**Key events:** Created in the image of God (1:26–27); formed from dust, breath of life (2:7); placed in the garden to work and keep it (2:15); commanded not to eat from the tree of knowledge (2:16–17); named the animals (2:19–20); deep sleep, side/rib taken (2:21); ate from the forbidden tree (3:6); expelled from Eden (3:23–24); knew Eve, begot Cain (4:1); begot Seth at age 130 (5:3); died at 930 (5:5)

**Familiar name:** Adam
**Profession:** farmer / steward of the garden (Gen 2:15; 3:23; 4:2)
**Social class:** not applicable (first human; no social hierarchy stated)
**Hometown:** Garden of Eden
**Places lived:** Garden of Eden; then the ground (*adamah*) east of Eden (Gen 3:23–24)
**Age when became father:** 130 (at Seth's birth, Gen 5:3; earlier sons Cain and Abel have no stated age)
**Cause of death:** not stated (Gen 5:5 simply records "and he died")
**Siblings:** none
**Archaeological evidence:** No direct archaeological evidence
**Extra-biblical mentions:** Josephus, *Antiquities* I.1–2; Quran, Sura 2:30–39 (Adam as khalifa); 2 Esdras 3:4–7; Life of Adam and Eve (Second Temple apocrypha)
**Historicity status:** LITERARY
**Books appearing in:** Genesis; 1 Chronicles 1:1; Luke 3:38; Romans 5:12–14; 1 Corinthians 15:22, 45; 1 Timothy 2:13–14; Jude 1:14
**Character arc:** Created in perfect relationship with YHWH, he breaks the one command given to him and is expelled; he continues farming in exile but loses direct divine intimacy.
**Year from creation:** 0 (by definition, AM 1 in the MT's own reckoning)
**Year from creation end:** 930

**Note:** The text uses *ha-adam* (the human, with article) through Gen 2–4. The transition to proper-name "Adam" (anarthrous) occurs at Gen 4:25 and is locked at 5:1. See editorial-log entry 081.

**Familiar name:** Enoch
**Profession:** not stated
**Social class:** not stated
**Hometown:** not stated
**Places lived:** not specified; "walked with God" suggests a unique relationship transcending geography
**Age when became father:** 65 (at Methuselah's birth, Gen 5:21)
**Cause of death:** not applicable — "God took him" (Gen 5:24); no death recorded
**Siblings:** "sons and daughters" of Jared implied (Gen 5:19)

**Year from creation end:** 987 (year of being "taken," not death)

**Note:** The formula "and he died" is absent. The 365-year lifespan equals the number of days in a solar year. Enoch is the seventh from Adam in the Sethite line. A Cainite Enoch (son of Cain) also appears at Gen 4:17 — different person, same name.
