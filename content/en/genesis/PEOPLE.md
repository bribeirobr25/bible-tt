# Genesis — People and Genealogy
## The Transparent Translation (TT)

---

**Book:** Genesis
**Language:** English
**Ruleset:** v3.0 (Rule 29 governs this file)
**Status:** provisional

---

**Scope:** Genesis 1--12. Major figures only. Genealogical data drawn from the Masoretic Text (BHS). Birth/death years are calculated from the MT's own internal arithmetic (Anno Mundi, year from creation). Where the text does not state a figure, the field reads "not stated." Transliterations follow TT convention (Rules 4, 25).

**Abbreviations:** AM = Anno Mundi (year from creation, per MT arithmetic). Gen = Genesis. v./vv. = verse(s).

## Adam (Adam)

**Meaning:** "human" / "of the ground" (*adam* from *adamah*)
**Origin:** CREATED
**Birth year:** AM 1 (by definition)
**Death year:** AM 930 (Gen 5:5)
**Lifespan:** 930 years (Gen 5:5)
**Father:** none (formed by YHWH Elohim from dust of the ground, Gen 2:7)
**Mother:** none
**Spouse(s):** Chava
**Children:** Qayin, Hevel, Shet; "sons and daughters" (Gen 5:4)
**Location(s):** Garden of Eden; expelled eastward (Gen 3:24); the ground (*adamah*)
**First mention:** Gen 1:26--27 (as "human"); Gen 2:7 (formed); Gen 5:1 (as proper name "Adam")
**Mentioned in:** Gen 1:26--27; 2:7--25; 3:1--24; 4:1, 25; 5:1--5
**Key events:** Created in the image of God (1:26--27); formed from dust, breath of life (2:7); placed in the garden to work and keep it (2:15); commanded not to eat from the tree of knowledge (2:16--17); named the animals (2:19--20); deep sleep, side/rib taken (2:21); ate from the forbidden tree (3:6); expelled from Eden (3:23--24); knew Chava, begot Qayin (4:1); begot Shet at age 130 (5:3); died at 930 (5:5)

**Note:** The text uses *ha-adam* (the human, with article) through Gen 2--4. The transition to proper-name "Adam" (anarthrous) occurs at Gen 4:25 and is locked at 5:1. See editorial-log entry 081.

## Chava (Eve)

**Meaning:** "living one" / "mother of all the living" (Gen 3:20; wordplay on *chay*, life)
**Origin:** CREATED (built from Adam's side/rib by YHWH Elohim, Gen 2:21--22)
**Birth year:** not stated (created during AM 1, no separate date given)
**Death year:** not stated
**Lifespan:** not stated
**Father:** none
**Mother:** none
**Spouse(s):** Adam
**Children:** Qayin (Gen 4:1); Hevel (Gen 4:2); Shet (Gen 4:25); implied others
**Location(s):** Garden of Eden; expelled with Adam (Gen 3:23--24)
**First mention:** Gen 2:22 (as "woman"); Gen 3:20 (named Chava by the human)
**Mentioned in:** Gen 2:21--25; 3:1--20; 4:1--2, 25
**Key events:** Built from Adam's side/rib (2:21--22); "bone from my bones" (2:23); conversed with the serpent (3:1--5); ate from the tree and gave to her man (3:6); toil in pregnancy and childbearing pronounced (3:16); named Chava by the human (3:20); bore Qayin (4:1); bore Hevel (4:2); bore Shet (4:25)

**Note:** The TT renders the name as "Chava" (Gen 3:20, 4:1) per Hebrew transliteration. Traditional English "Eve" derives from LXX *Eua* via Latin.

**Children:** Yaval (by Adah, Gen 4:20); Yuval (by Adah, Gen 4:21); Tuval-Qayin (by Tsillah, Gen 4:22); Na'amah (by Tsillah, Gen 4:22)

## Avram (Abram)

**Meaning:** "exalted father" (*av* + *ram*)
**Origin:** BORN
**Birth year:** AM 1948 (per MT arithmetic: Shem 1558 + Arpakhshad at 100 [=1658] + Shelach at 35 [=1693] + Ever at 30 [=1723] + Peleg at 34 [=1757] + Re'u at 30 [=1787] + Serug at 32 [=1819] + Nachor at 30 [=1849] + Terach at 29 [=1878] + Avram at 70 [=1948]; but see note)
**Death year:** not stated within Gen 1--12
**Lifespan:** not stated within Gen 1--12
**Father:** Terach (Gen 11:26--27)
**Mother:** not stated
**Spouse(s):** Sarai (Gen 11:29; 12:11--13)
**Children:** none yet within Gen 1--12 (Sarai is barren, Gen 11:30)
**Location(s):** Ur of the Kasdim (Gen 11:28, 31); Charan (Gen 11:31; 12:4); land of Kenaan (12:5); Shekhem, oak of Moreh (12:6); hill country between Beyt-El and Ai (12:8); the Negev (12:9); Egypt (12:10--20)
**First mention:** Gen 11:26
**Mentioned in:** Gen 11:26--32; 12:1--20
**Key events:** Born to Terach (11:26); married Sarai (11:29); departed from Ur with Terach, Sarai, and Lot toward Kenaan, settled in Charan (11:31); called by YHWH -- *lekh lekha* (12:1); received five-fold promise (12:2--3); departed Charan at age 75 (12:4); arrived in Kenaan (12:5); passed through to Shekhem, YHWH appeared and promised the land to his seed (12:6--7); built altars at Shekhem and between Beyt-El and Ai (12:7--8); called on the name of YHWH (12:8); went down to Egypt due to famine (12:10); told Sarai to say she was his sister (12:11--13); Pharaoh struck with plagues on account of Sarai (12:17); rebuked by Pharaoh and sent away (12:18--20)

**Note:** If Avram is Terach's firstborn (listed first at 11:26) and Terach was 70 at the birth of his first son, then Avram was born AM 1948. However, Gen 12:4 states Avram was 75 when he left Charan, and Gen 11:32 states Terach died in Charan at 205. If Avram left after Terach's death, Avram was born when Terach was 130 (205 - 75), not 70. The text does not harmonize these figures. Listed here is the calculation assuming Terach was 70 at Avram's birth, but the tension is noted.
