## READING GUIDE

**Main Text:** Primary translation — readable but faithful to Greek structure. **Notes:** Essential Greek features immediately below each verse.

**How the text is marked:**
- *Words in italics* — added for English grammar (not in the Greek text)
- {a:wind/spirit} — a word the Greek leaves open to two meanings, both kept
- @@"…"@@ — direct speech of God or the holy wind/spirit

Notes are tagged by type — **Critical · Lexical · Grammatical · Theological** — each shown with its colour (see the legend at the top of the Notes view).

**This is the opening of 1 Peter — the corpus's first *letter*.** It is a circular epistle to scattered believers — "elect exiles of the dispersion" — across five provinces of Asia Minor (1:1, provinces that overlap the Acts 2:9 pilgrim list). Chapter 1 has the epistolary **salutation** (vv.1-2), then a single soaring Greek sentence of **blessing** (vv.3-12) on the new birth, the living hope, the imperishable inheritance, and the salvation the prophets searched out; then the first **exhortations** — to holiness (vv.13-21) and to love from a pure heart (vv.22-25). **Note on speech-marking (Rule 30):** 1 Peter is the apostle's exhortation in his own voice — there is no narrated divine speech; only God's first-person word *inside* an OT citation is marked (1:16, "I am holy," from Leviticus). Proper names follow TT transliteration: Kefa (Peter), Yeshua (Jesus).

## CHAPTER OVERVIEW

**What happens:** Peter writes as an apostle of Jesus the Messiah to the elect exiles of the dispersion scattered across Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia — chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, by the sanctifying work of the wind/spirit, for obedience and the sprinkling of Jesus's blood (vv.1-2). He breaks into blessing: God has given a new birth into a living hope through the resurrection, and an imperishable inheritance kept in the heavens for them, who are guarded by God's power for a salvation ready to be revealed (vv.3-5). In this they rejoice, though now grieved by various trials, so that their tested faith — more precious than perishable gold — may be found to praise and glory at the revelation of Jesus; whom, not having seen, they love (vv.6-9). The prophets searched out this salvation, the wind/spirit of the Messiah in them pointing ahead to the Messiah's sufferings and the glories to follow — things now announced through those who preached the good news by the holy wind/spirit, things angels long to look into (vv.10-12). Therefore: gird the loins of your mind, be sober, set your hope fully on the coming grace (v.13); as children of obedience, be holy as the One who called you is holy — "be holy, for I am holy" (vv.14-16); conduct yourselves in reverent fear through your time as exiles, knowing you were ransomed not with silver or gold but with the precious blood of the Messiah, a lamb without blemish, foreknown before the foundation of the world and revealed in these last times (vv.17-21). Having purified your souls in obedience to the truth, love one another earnestly from a pure heart, having been born again through the living and abiding word of God — for "all flesh is grass… but the word of the Lord endures forever" (vv.22-25).

**Key themes:** The epistolary salutation and the triune-shaped election (foreknowledge of the Father / sanctification of the wind/spirit / sprinkling of the blood); the **new birth (anagennaō)** into a living hope; the imperishable inheritance "kept in the heavens"; tested faith more precious than gold, refined by fire; loving an unseen Lord; the prophets and the "wind/spirit of the Messiah" foretelling the sufferings-then-glories pattern; the call to holiness grounded in God's own holiness (Leviticus); the **ransom** by the Messiah's blood, the lamb without blemish, foreknown before the world's foundation; the identity of the readers as **exiles/sojourners (paroikia)** living in reverent fear; the new birth through the living, abiding word, set against the withering of all flesh (Isaiah 40).

**Watch for:** This is a letter — vv.3-12 are one periodic sentence in Greek; the TT keeps its subordinated, suspended structure. The salutation's three phrases (vv.1-2) carry a triune shape; the TT renders the Greek without importing later dogmatic vocabulary (Rule 3). *Pneuma* keeps the wind/spirit slash (1:2, 11, 12). At 1:11 "the wind/spirit of the Messiah" in the prophets is a notable claim — rendered as the Greek has it. The Leviticus citation (1:16) is God's first-person word — marked @@…@@. *Kyrios* at 1:25 (Isaiah 40:8) = YHWH (Option C: "the Lord"). "Exiles… dispersion… sojourning" (*parepidēmoi / diaspora / paroikia*) is the letter's identity keyword — kept literal.

**Connection:** The chapter is steeped in the Hebrew Bible. "Be holy, for I am holy" (1:16) cites Leviticus 11:44-45; 19:2 — the holiness code grounding Israel's vocation, now applied to the scattered believers. "Gird the loins" (1:13) echoes the Exodus posture (Ex 12:11) and the prophets. The ransom by a "lamb without blemish" (1:19) draws on the Passover lamb (Ex 12) and the sacrificial code (Lev). "All flesh is grass… the word of the Lord endures" (1:24-25) quotes Isaiah 40:6-8 — the opening of the prophecies of comfort, the same Isaiah 40 that frames John the Immerser in the gospels. The "dispersion" (diaspora) language casts the readers in Israel's exile identity.

## VERSE-BY-VERSE STUDY

### **Verse 1**

Kefa (Peter), an apostle of Yeshua *the* Messiah (Jesus), to *the* elect sojourners of *the* dispersion of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — THE LETTER OPENING: "ELECT SOJOURNERS OF THE DISPERSION"**
> - **ἐκλεκτοῖς παρεπιδήμοις διασπορᾶς** (*eklektois parepidēmois diasporas*) = "to *the* elect sojourners of *the* dispersion." The Greek epistolary salutation (sender → recipients → greeting). **παρεπίδημος** (*parepidēmos*) = "resident foreigner, sojourner, exile"; **διασπορά** (*diaspora*) = the scattering of Israel — the letter casts its readers in Israel's exile identity (cf. 1:17 *paroikia*; 2:11). The five provinces are Roman Asia Minor; the order suggests the letter-carrier's circuit. → For the recipient communities and the epistolary form, see the Introduction (§B, §D).

---

### **Verse 2**

according to *the* foreknowledge of God *the* Father, in sanctification of *the* {a:wind/spirit}, for obedience and sprinkling of *the* blood of Yeshua *the* Messiah (Jesus): may grace and peace be multiplied to you.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — THE TRIUNE-SHAPED ELECTION (the salutation crux)**
> - Three phrases govern the election: **κατὰ πρόγνωσιν θεοῦ πατρός** ("according to *the* foreknowledge of God *the* Father"), **ἐν ἁγιασμῷ πνεύματος** ("in sanctification of *the* {a:wind/spirit}"), **εἰς ὑπακοὴν καὶ ῥαντισμὸν αἵματος** ("for obedience and sprinkling of *the* blood"). The TT renders the Greek's Father/wind-spirit/blood structure **without importing the later dogmatic vocabulary** "the Trinity" (Rule 3). **ῥαντισμὸν αἵματος** ("sprinkling of blood") echoes the covenant ritual (Ex 24:8). → See the editorial-log entry (1-peter.md P-008) and Companion §A.
>
> 🔵 **"MAY GRACE AND PEACE BE MULTIPLIED" — *PLĒTHYNTHEIĒ***
> - **χάρις ὑμῖν καὶ εἰρήνη πληθυνθείη** (*charis… eirēnē plēthyntheiē*) = the epistolary health-wish, optative ("may it be multiplied"). *Charis* (grace/favor) + *eirēnē* (peace, Hebrew *shalom*) — the Greco-Roman *chairein* greeting reshaped.

---

### **Verse 3**

Blessed *be* the God and Father of our Lord Yeshua *the* Messiah (Jesus), who according to his great mercy gave us new birth into a living hope through *the* resurrection of Yeshua (Jesus) from *the* dead,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "GAVE US NEW BIRTH" — *ANAGENNĒSAS***
> - **ὁ… ἀναγεννήσας ἡμᾶς εἰς ἐλπίδα ζῶσαν** (*ho anagennēsas hēmas eis elpida zōsan*) = "who gave us new birth into a living hope." **ἀναγεννάω** (*anagennaō*, only here and 1:23 in the GS) = "beget again / give new birth." The blessing-form **εὐλογητός** ("Blessed *be*") opens a single periodic sentence running through v.12. *Kyrios* here ("our Lord Yeshua") = the title of Jesus.

---

### **Verse 4**

into an inheritance imperishable and undefiled and unfading, kept in *the* heavens for you

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"IMPERISHABLE AND UNDEFILED AND UNFADING" — THREE ALPHA-PRIVATIVES**
> - **ἄφθαρτον καὶ ἀμίαντον καὶ ἀμάραντον** (*aphtharton… amianton… amaranton*) = three rhyming alpha-privative adjectives (im-perishable, un-defiled, un-fading) — a deliberate sound-pattern the TT keeps as a triad. **κληρονομία** ("inheritance") evokes the land-inheritance of Israel, now "kept in *the* heavens."

---

### **Verse 5**

who are guarded by *the* power of God through faith, for a salvation ready to be revealed in *the* last time.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"GUARDED" — *PHROUROUMENOUS***
> - **φρουρουμένους** (*phrouroumenous*) = "being guarded/garrisoned" (a military term) — God's power garrisons the believers. **σωτηρίαν ἑτοίμην ἀποκαλυφθῆναι ἐν καιρῷ ἐσχάτῳ** = "a salvation ready to be revealed in *the* last time" — salvation as a future unveiling.

---

### **Verse 6**

In this you rejoice, *though* now for a little *while*, if *it must* be, *you have been* grieved by various trials,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"VARIOUS TRIALS" — *POIKILOIS PEIRASMOIS***
> - **ποικίλοις πειρασμοῖς** (*poikilois peirasmois*) = "various / many-colored trials." The suffering of the readers is the letter's pastoral occasion. **ἀγαλλιᾶσθε** ("you rejoice") may be indicative or imperative; the TT reads indicative (their present joy amid trial).

---

### **Verse 7**

so that the proving of your faith — more precious than gold that perishes, *yet* being tested through fire — may be found for praise and glory and honor at *the* revelation of Yeshua *the* Messiah (Jesus);

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"THE PROVING OF YOUR FAITH" — *TO DOKIMION***
> - **τὸ δοκίμιον ὑμῶν τῆς πίστεως** (*to dokimion… tēs pisteōs*) = "the proving/tested-genuineness of your faith." The gold-refined-by-fire image (cf. Prov 17:3; Mal 3:3) makes trial the assay of faith. **ἀποκάλυψις** ("revelation") of Jesus = his future unveiling (vv.5, 13).

---

### **Verse 8**

whom, not having seen, you love; in whom, not seeing *him* now but believing, you rejoice with joy inexpressible and glorified,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"JOY INEXPRESSIBLE AND GLORIFIED" — *CHARA ANEKLALĒTŌ***
> - **χαρᾷ ἀνεκλαλήτῳ καὶ δεδοξασμένῃ** (*chara aneklalētō kai dedoxasmenē*) = "joy inexpressible and glorified." Loving and believing in an **unseen** Lord (cf. Jn 20:29) is the readers' situation — faith, not sight.

---

### **Verse 9**

receiving the outcome of your faith, *the* salvation of *your* souls.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"THE SALVATION OF SOULS" — *SŌTĒRIAN PSYCHŌN***
> - **σωτηρίαν ψυχῶν** (*sōtērian psychōn*) = "salvation of souls." **ψυχή** = "soul / life / self" (not a disembodied part — the whole person; cf. 3:20 "eight souls"). **τέλος** ("outcome/end/goal") of faith.

---

### **Verse 10**

Concerning this salvation, *the* prophets searched and inquired *carefully*, who prophesied concerning the grace *coming* to you,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"SEARCHED AND INQUIRED" — *EXEZĒTĒSAN KAI EXĒRAUNĒSAN***
> - **ἐξεζήτησαν καὶ ἐξηραύνησαν** (*exezētēsan kai exēraunēsan*) = "searched out and investigated *carefully*" — an emphatic doublet. The HB prophets are cast as inquirers into the salvation now arrived; vv.10-12 are the chapter's statement of how Scripture pointed forward.

---

### **Verse 11**

inquiring into what or what manner of time the {a:wind/spirit} of *the* Messiah in them was indicating, testifying beforehand to the sufferings *destined* for the Messiah and the glories after these.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "THE WIND/SPIRIT OF THE MESSIAH" IN THE PROPHETS**
> - **τὸ ἐν αὐτοῖς πνεῦμα Χριστοῦ προμαρτυρόμενον** (*to en autois pneuma Christou promartyromenon*) = "the {a:wind/spirit} of *the* Messiah in them, testifying beforehand." The claim is that the Messiah's *pneuma* was active in the prophets, signaling **τὰ εἰς Χριστὸν παθήματα καὶ τὰς μετὰ ταῦτα δόξας** ("the sufferings *destined* for the Messiah and the glories after these") — the suffering-then-glory pattern (cf. Lk 24:26; Acts 3:18). The TT keeps the wind/spirit slash. **[theological — the pre-existent activity claim: rendered as the Greek has it; PROBABLE.]**

---

### **Verse 12**

To them it was revealed that not for themselves, but for you, they were serving these *things*, which now have been announced to you through those who preached the good news to you by *the* holy {a:wind/spirit} sent from heaven — into which *things* angels long to look.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"ANGELS LONG TO LOOK INTO" — *PARAKYPSAI***
> - **εἰς ἃ ἐπιθυμοῦσιν ἄγγελοι παρακύψαι** (*eis ha epithymousin angeloi parakypsai*) = "into which *things* angels long to stoop-to-look" (*parakyptō*, to bend over to peer in, as into a tomb, Lk 24:12). The prophets served a later generation; even angels desire to gaze into the gospel. **εὐαγγελισαμένων** = "those who preached the good news" (*euangelion*-cognate).

---

### **Verse 13**

Therefore, having girded the loins of your mind, being sober, set your hope completely on the grace being brought to you at *the* revelation of Yeshua *the* Messiah (Jesus).

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "GIRD THE LOINS OF YOUR MIND" (the first imperative)**
> - **ἀναζωσάμενοι τὰς ὀσφύας τῆς διανοίας ὑμῶν** (*anazōsamenoi tas osphyas tēs dianoias hymōn*) = "having girded the loins of your mind" — the Exodus posture of readiness (Ex 12:11, tucking the robe to move) made interior. **νήφοντες** ("being sober") + **τελείως ἐλπίσατε** ("hope completely") — the body's first main imperative; everything before (vv.3-12) was ground, this is the call.

---

### **Verse 14**

As children of obedience, not being conformed to the former desires *that were yours* in your ignorance,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"CHILDREN OF OBEDIENCE" — A SEMITIC IDIOM**
> - **ὡς τέκνα ὑπακοῆς** (*hōs tekna hypakoēs*) = "as children of obedience" — a Hebraic "children of X" idiom (= obedient children). **μὴ συσχηματιζόμενοι** ("not being conformed/configured to," cf. Rom 12:2) the desires of their pre-conversion **ἄγνοια** ("ignorance").

---

### **Verse 15**

but according to the holy *One* who called you, you yourselves also be holy in all *your* conduct,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"IN ALL CONDUCT" — *ANASTROPHĒ***
> - **ἐν πάσῃ ἀναστροφῇ** (*en pasē anastrophē*) = "in all *your* conduct / way of life." *Anastrophē* (manner of living) is a 1 Peter keyword (1:18; 2:12; 3:1,2,16). Holiness is conduct-shaped, grounded in "the holy *One* who called you."

---

### **Verse 16**

because it is written, @@"Be holy, because I *am* holy."@@

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — LEVITICUS 11:44-45 / 19:2 (divine first-person speech)**
> - **Ἅγιοι ἔσεσθε, ὅτι ἐγὼ ἅγιος** (*Hagioi esesthe, hoti egō hagios*) = "Be holy, because I *am* holy" — a citation of God's own first-person word in Leviticus (11:44-45; 19:2; 20:7), so it is marked **@@…@@** (Rule 30 / A-004). The holiness grounding Israel's vocation is applied to the scattered believers. The copula *am* is added in italics (Greek verbless). → Companion §A; PROPHECY (Lev → 1 Pet 1:16).

---

### **Verse 17**

And if you call upon *as* Father the *One* judging impartially according to each one's work, conduct yourselves in fear *during* the time of your sojourning,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"THE TIME OF YOUR SOJOURNING" — *PAROIKIAS***
> - **τὸν τῆς παροικίας ὑμῶν χρόνον** (*ton tēs paroikias hymōn chronon*) = "the time of your sojourning / exile-residence." *Paroikia* (resident-alien status) reinforces the exile identity of 1:1. God judges **ἀπροσωπολήμπτως** ("without partiality / not receiving faces") — Tier-1 reverent fear, not dread.

---

### **Verse 18**

knowing that not with perishable *things* — silver or gold — were you ransomed from your futile way of life handed down from *the* fathers,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"YOU WERE RANSOMED" — *ELYTRŌTHĒTE***
> - **ἐλυτρώθητε** (*elytrōthēte*) = "you were ransomed/redeemed" (*lytroō*, the redemption-price word; cf. the Exodus redemption). **ματαίας… ἀναστροφῆς πατροπαραδότου** = "futile way of life handed down from *the* fathers" — ancestral custom relativized.

---

### **Verse 19**

but with *the* precious blood, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot, of *the* Messiah,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "A LAMB WITHOUT BLEMISH" (Passover + sacrifice)**
> - **τιμίῳ αἵματι ὡς ἀμνοῦ ἀμώμου καὶ ἀσπίλου Χριστοῦ** (*timiō haimati hōs amnou amōmou… Christou*) = "with *the* precious blood, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot, of *the* Messiah." **ἀμνός** ("lamb") + **ἄμωμος** ("unblemished," the LXX sacrificial-standard word) evoke the Passover lamb (Ex 12:5) and Isaiah 53:7; the ransom-price is the Messiah's blood, not silver/gold (v.18). → PROPHECY (Ex 12 / Isa 53 → 1 Pet 1:19).

---

### **Verse 20**

foreknown before *the* foundation of *the* world but revealed in *the* last of the times for your *sake*,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"FOREKNOWN BEFORE THE FOUNDATION OF THE WORLD"**
> - **προεγνωσμένου… πρὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου** (*proegnōsmenou pro katabolēs kosmou*) = "foreknown before *the* foundation of *the* world." The same *prognōsis* root as 1:2; the Messiah's redemptive role is pre-temporal, "revealed (**φανερωθέντος**) in *the* last of the times."

---

### **Verse 21**

who through him *are* faithful toward God, the *One* who raised him from *the* dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope might be in God.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"RAISED HIM AND GAVE HIM GLORY"**
> - **τὸν ἐγείραντα αὐτὸν ἐκ νεκρῶν καὶ δόξαν αὐτῷ δόντα** = "who raised him from *the* dead and gave him glory" — the suffering-then-glory pattern of v.11, now of the readers' faith: "so that your faith and hope might be in God."

---

### **Verse 22**

Having purified your souls in the obedience of the truth for sincere brotherly love, love one another earnestly from a pure heart,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **"BROTHERLY LOVE" + "LOVE EARNESTLY" — *PHILADELPHIA / AGAPĒSATE***
> - **φιλαδελφίαν ἀνυπόκριτον… ἀλλήλους ἀγαπήσατε ἐκτενῶς** = "sincere **brotherly love**… love one another **earnestly**." Both love-words appear: *philadelphia* (sibling affection) as the goal, *agapaō* (committed love) as the command; **ἐκτενῶς** ("earnestly, fervently, stretched-out"). The purified soul issues in concrete community love.

---

### **Verse 23**

having been born again, not from perishable seed but imperishable, through *the* living and abiding word of God.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — "BORN AGAIN… THROUGH THE LIVING WORD" (*ANAGEGENNĒMENOI*)**
> - **ἀναγεγεννημένοι… διὰ λόγου ζῶντος θεοῦ καὶ μένοντος** = "having been born again… through *the* living and abiding word of God." The new-birth verb of 1:3 returns; the agent is the **λόγος** ("word") of God, living and abiding — bridging to the Isaiah 40 citation (vv.24-25, where "the word of *the* Lord endures"). **ἄφθαρτος** ("imperishable") seed ties back to the imperishable inheritance (v.4).

---

### **Verse 24**

For "all flesh *is* like grass, and all its glory like *the* flower of grass; the grass withered, and the flower fell off,

> **Notes**
>
> 🔵 **ISAIAH 40:6-7 — "ALL FLESH IS GRASS"**
> - **πᾶσα σὰρξ ὡς χόρτος** (*pasa sarx hōs chortos*) = "all flesh *is* like grass" (Isa 40:6-7 LXX). The transience of "all flesh" and its "glory" sets up the contrast with the enduring word (v.25). Human-voice citation (Isaiah's), so not divine-speech-marked. → PROPHECY (Isa 40:6-8 → 1 Pet 1:24-25).

### **Verse 25**

but the word of *the* Lord endures into the age." And this is the word that was preached as good news to you.

> **Notes**
>
> 🔴 **CRITICAL — ISAIAH 40:8 (κύριος = YHWH) APPLIED TO THE GOSPEL**
> - **τὸ δὲ ῥῆμα κυρίου μένει εἰς τὸν αἰῶνα** (*to de rhēma kyriou menei eis ton aiōna*) = "but the word of *the* Lord endures into the age." In Isaiah, *kyrios* = **YHWH** (Option C: "the Lord"); 1 Peter then identifies "the word of *the* Lord" with "the word that was preached as good news to you" — the gospel is that enduring word. **ῥῆμα** (here) / **λόγος** (v.23) both render "word." → PROPHECY (Isa 40:8 → 1 Pet 1:25).

## Glossary — 1 Peter 1

| Term | Greek | Meaning in context |
|------|-------|--------------------|
| **sojourners / dispersion** | παρεπίδημοι / διασπορά (*parepidēmoi / diaspora*) | Resident-foreigners; the scattering of Israel — the readers' exile identity (1:1, 17; cf. 2:11). |
| **foreknowledge** | πρόγνωσις (*prognōsis*) | God's prior knowing/choosing (1:2; cf. the Messiah "foreknown," 1:20). |
| **{a:wind/spirit}** | πνεῦμα (*pneuma*) | Wind / spirit / breath — the locked GS slash (1:2, 11, 12). |
| **new birth / born again** | ἀναγεννάω (*anagennaō*) | "Beget again" (1:3, 23) — only here in the GS; into a living hope, through the living word. |
| **inheritance** | κληρονομία (*klēronomia*) | The imperishable inheritance "kept in the heavens" (1:4); echoes Israel's land. |
| **the proving** | δοκίμιον (*dokimion*) | The tested genuineness of faith, refined like gold by fire (1:7). |
| **ransomed** | λυτρόω (*lytroō*) | Redeemed by a price — the Messiah's blood, not silver/gold (1:18-19). |
| **lamb without blemish** | ἀμνὸς ἄμωμος (*amnos amōmos*) | The Passover/sacrificial standard (Ex 12; Lev), applied to the Messiah (1:19). |
| **the Lord** | κύριος (*kyrios*) | Option C: "our Lord" Yeshua (1:3); YHWH in the Isaiah 40 citation (1:25). |
| **conduct / way of life** | ἀναστροφή (*anastrophē*) | A keyword — holy conduct (1:15, 18; 2:12; 3:1-2, 16). |
| **word** | λόγος / ῥῆμα (*logos / rhēma*) | The living, abiding word of God = the gospel preached (1:23, 25). |
